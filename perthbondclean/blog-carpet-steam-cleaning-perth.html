Carpet Steam Cleaning Perth — What Renters Need to Know Before Moving Out

Published May 2025  |  5 min read

Carpet steam cleaning is one of the most searched topics by Perth renters in the weeks before they move out — and for good reason. Get it wrong and your property manager can withhold bond money. Get it right and it's one less thing to worry about on settlement day.

This guide answers every common question Perth renters ask about carpet steam cleaning at the end of a tenancy.

Does My Lease Require Professional Carpet Steam Cleaning?

Check your lease agreement first. Many Perth residential leases include a specific clause requiring carpets to be professionally steam cleaned (sometimes called hot water extraction) before vacating. If your lease includes this clause, you must comply — and you'll typically need to provide a receipt from a licensed operator as proof.

Even if your lease doesn't specifically require professional steam cleaning, the Residential Tenancies Act 1987 (WA) requires you to return the property in a "reasonable state of cleanliness." If carpets are stained, odorous, or visibly dirty, a property manager can still claim against your bond for professional cleaning regardless of what the lease says.

What Is Carpet Steam Cleaning (Hot Water Extraction)?

Carpet steam cleaning — more accurately called hot water extraction — involves injecting hot water and cleaning solution deep into carpet fibres under pressure, then immediately extracting it along with loosened dirt, allergens, and stains. It's more effective than vacuuming or dry cleaning for removing deep-seated grime, pet odour, and stubborn stains.

A standard vacuuming as part of your bond clean will remove surface dirt but won't satisfy a lease requirement for professional steam cleaning, and won't remove stains or pet hair embedded in the fibres.

Can I Hire a Carpet Cleaner and Do It Myself?

Hardware stores and supermarkets in Perth rent out carpet cleaning machines for around $50–$80 per day. DIY can work for light surface cleaning, but there are real risks:

  • Consumer machines have far less suction power than professional equipment — carpets may not dry properly, causing mould
  • Using too much water or the wrong cleaning solution can damage carpet fibres or leave residue
  • If your lease requires "professional" steam cleaning, a DIY job may not satisfy the requirement — and you won't have a receipt from a licensed operator
  • Stubborn stains often remain after DIY cleaning

For the lease compliance and peace of mind, most Perth renters are better off booking a professional.

What About Pet Odour and Stains?

If you've had pets in the property, your property manager will check for pet odour and pet hair embedded in carpet. Standard steam cleaning addresses most issues, but heavily affected carpets may require specialist pet odour treatment. Be upfront about this when requesting a quote — it affects both the method used and the price.

When Should I Book?

Book at least a week before your move-out date. Perth bond cleaners are particularly busy at month-ends and on Fridays. Carpets need 4–8 hours to dry fully after steam cleaning — factor this into your timeline, especially if the property inspection is the same day or next morning.

The ideal sequence is: bond clean first, then carpet steam cleaning in the final hours before vacating. This ensures floors are clean before the carpets are done, and the property is left in perfect condition.

Do I Need to Keep the Receipt?

Yes. Keep your receipt from the carpet cleaner. Most property managers in Perth will ask for it as proof that professional cleaning was carried out. If you use Perth Bond Clean, we can arrange carpet steam cleaning as part of your booking and provide a single receipt covering both services.
