Complete Bond Cleaning Checklist

Every clean follows the REIWA-aligned checklist. Here's exactly what we cover in every room.

🍳 Kitchen

  • Oven interior deep cleaned (racks, glass, element)
  • Grill/griller tray degreased
  • Rangehood filters cleaned & degreased
  • Stovetop/cooktop degreased
  • Splashback cleaned
  • Benchtops wiped down
  • Sink & tapware cleaned & polished
  • Cupboards & drawers inside and out
  • Dishwasher interior & door seals
  • Exhaust fan cover cleaned
  • Light fittings dusted
  • Floor vacuumed & mopped

🚿 Bathrooms & Toilets

  • Shower screens descaled & polished
  • Shower tiles scrubbed
  • Grout cleaned & mould treated
  • Bath cleaned (if applicable)
  • Vanity, basin & tapware polished
  • Mirror cleaned streak-free
  • Toilet bowl, seat, lid & base sanitised
  • Toilet cistern wiped down
  • Exhaust fan cover cleaned
  • Towel rails & toilet roll holder wiped
  • Floor tiles scrubbed & mopped
  • Skirting boards wiped

🛏️ Bedrooms

  • Wardrobes inside & out — shelves, rails & tracks
  • Mirrored robes cleaned streak-free
  • All surfaces dusted
  • Window sills, ledges & tracks cleaned
  • Windows cleaned inside
  • Skirting boards wiped
  • Light fittings & switches cleaned
  • Carpet vacuumed (steam clean optional)

🏠 Living Areas & General

  • All walls spot-cleaned
  • Doors, door frames & handles wiped
  • Light switches & power points cleaned
  • Air conditioning filters cleaned
  • Blinds dusted (standard)
  • Ceiling fans dusted
  • Smoke detectors dusted
  • Skirting boards throughout
  • All floors vacuumed & mopped
  • Cobwebs removed (interior)

🌿 Outdoor & Garage

  • Balcony / alfresco swept & mopped
  • Outdoor glass & sliding door tracks
  • Cobwebs removed (exterior)
  • Garage floor swept
  • Garage walls & shelving wiped
  • Rubbish removed from property

Why Use a Professional Bond Cleaner?

Many tenants attempt to clean the property themselves to save money — but this often leads to failed inspections, arguments with property managers, and ultimately a deduction from the bond anyway.

Professional bond cleaners know exactly what Perth property managers look for. They use industrial-grade equipment and chemicals to achieve results that simply can't be replicated with household products. A professional clean typically costs $300–$600 — significantly less than having bond money withheld or facing a dispute.
