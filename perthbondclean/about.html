About Perth Bond Clean

We exist to make moving out in Perth less stressful — connecting renters with trusted, insured bond cleaners who get the job done right, every time.

Our Story

Perth Bond Clean was founded after our team experienced first-hand what too many Perth renters go through: hiring a cheap, unvetted cleaner who did a substandard job, failing the exit inspection, and losing hundreds of dollars in bond deductions.

We built Perth Bond Clean to solve that problem. We vet every cleaner in our network for experience, insurance, and police clearance — and we back every clean with our 100% bond back guarantee.

Since 2020, we've helped over 1,200 Perth renters pass their exit inspections and get their full bond back. Our network covers the entire Perth metro area, from Joondalup in the north to Mandurah in the south.

Why We Exist

The Perth rental market is competitive. With vacancy rates low and property managers under pressure, exit inspections are stricter than ever. A single missed area on a bond clean can result in hundreds of dollars being withheld from your bond — sometimes unfairly.

We believe every Perth renter deserves access to a high-quality, affordable bond clean that gives them the best chance of passing their inspection on the first attempt. That's why we built this service — and it's why we back every single clean with our bond back guarantee.
