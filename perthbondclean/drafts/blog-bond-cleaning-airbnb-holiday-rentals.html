Bond Cleaning for Airbnb & Holiday Rentals Perth

Published 2026  |  5 min read

Short-term and holiday rentals in Perth operate differently to standard residential leases, but exit cleaning requirements can be just as demanding — sometimes more so. Whether you're an Airbnb host who's been living in your property, a tenant vacating a short-term furnished apartment, or a holiday rental guest dealing with end-of-stay cleaning expectations, the rules and standards differ from a traditional bond clean. Here's what you need to know.

Does a Standard Bond Clean Apply to Holiday Rentals?

Strictly speaking, the residential tenancy bond framework under the Residential Tenancies Act 1987 (WA) applies to residential leases — not short-term holiday bookings. However, many Airbnb and short-stay properties in Perth use booking contracts that include exit cleaning obligations. The key differences are:

  • No formal bond lodgement with the Bond Administrator — instead, a security deposit or cleaning fee held by the platform
  • No REIWA Property Condition Report — but photographic records are used as evidence
  • Cleaning to "hotel standard" rather than REIWA checklist standard (though these largely overlap)
  • Faster turnaround times — the property may need to be ready for new guests within hours

When a Bond Clean IS Applicable

Some Perth properties are rented on 12-month leases for "holiday accommodation" or furnished executive stays — these ARE subject to the full Residential Tenancies Act and require a proper bond clean at exit, including REIWA inspection, professional carpet steam cleaning, and all standard bond clean areas.

What Airbnb Hosts Typically Require

For short-stay properties, the exit clean standard is usually set by the host's house rules. Common requirements include:

  • Kitchen cleaned to hotel standard — oven, stovetop, surfaces, and appliances
  • All bathrooms spotless — shower, vanity, toilet, and floors
  • All floors vacuumed/swept and mopped
  • Rubbish removed to outdoor bins
  • Linen and towels stripped (sometimes laundered by guest)
  • BBQ or outdoor area left clean

Professional Short-Stay Cleaning vs Bond Cleaning

Professional bond cleaning companies can handle both short-stay and lease-end cleans. If you're vacating a fully furnished Airbnb apartment and need a professional-standard clean, the scope is similar to a standard bond clean — it just may not require the full REIWA checklist. However, furnishings (couches, beds, dining furniture) are not part of a standard bond clean and may require a separate quote.

Protecting Your Security Deposit

Whether you're a guest or a longer-term furnished tenant, the rules for protecting your deposit are the same as for a bond: photograph everything at check-in, report pre-existing damage immediately in writing, and ensure the exit clean is professionally completed. In a dispute, photographic evidence and communication records are your best protection.
