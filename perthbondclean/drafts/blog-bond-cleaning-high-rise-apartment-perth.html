Bond Cleaning a High-Rise Apartment Perth

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Vacating a high-rise apartment in Perth comes with specific bond cleaning challenges that ground-floor renters don't face. From building access logistics to the question of external windows and balconies, here's what you need to know.

What's Included in a High-Rise Bond Clean

The REIWA standard applies regardless of what floor you're on. A bond clean for a high-rise apartment covers:

  • Full kitchen clean — oven (if present), rangehood, surfaces, cupboards inside and out
  • Bathroom and ensuite — shower screen, tiles, vanity, toilet, and exhaust fan
  • All floors — vacuumed and mopped
  • All windows interior — glass, sills, and tracks
  • Balcony — floor swept and washed, balustrade and sliding door tracks cleaned
  • Air conditioning filters — split-system filters washed and dried
  • Laundry or washer area

External Windows — A Special Case

In high-rise apartments, external window cleaning is typically handled by the building's facilities management team, not the tenant. Most strata schemes include common area maintenance that covers external glass cleaning. Check your building's strata rules and confirm with the property manager what, if anything, you are responsible for on the outside of external glass.

Balconies in High-Rise Buildings

Balconies in high-rise apartments can accumulate significant bird droppings, salt deposits, and airborne grime depending on the building's orientation and height. The balcony floor must be swept and mopped, the balustrade wiped, and the sliding door and track cleaned. Bird droppings on balcony floors can be difficult to remove and may require a dedicated cleaning product.

Building Access for Cleaners

Coordinate building access in advance. Most high-rise buildings require:

  • Advance notice to the building manager for trade access
  • A lift booking for equipment movement
  • A visitor parking arrangement for the cleaning team's vehicle
  • Swipe card or fob access provided to the cleaner

Inform your bond cleaner of any building access requirements when booking — most professional cleaners are experienced with high-rise access but need the information in advance.

Compact Apartment Layouts

High-rise apartments are often compact, which can create specific cleaning challenges: limited bench space near the oven and sink, narrow bathroom layouts where manoeuvring cleaning equipment is difficult, and small laundry cupboards that accumulate lint. A professional cleaner experienced with apartment bonds will be accustomed to these constraints.
