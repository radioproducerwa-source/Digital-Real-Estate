Bond Cleaning Shenton Park Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Shenton Park is a green, leafy suburb about 7 kilometres from the Perth CBD, bordered by Shenton Park lake and close to the University of Western Australia. It attracts a mix of academics, families, and professionals. The rental stock is diverse — older character homes, converted apartments, newer townhouses, and a number of modern apartment blocks developed since the early 2000s.

Character Homes — Specific Requirements

Shenton Park's older character homes have features that require extra attention:

  • Jarrah or pine floorboards — require appropriate cleaning products and light mopping only
  • High ceilings and ornate cornices — cobwebs collect in crevices and need careful removal
  • Sash windows — channels and sills accumulate years of grime
  • Older kitchen layouts — open shelving and older appliances require thorough cleaning
  • Leadlight windows (where present) — clean frame and sill only; handle glass with care

Modern Apartments and Townhouses

Newer developments near Stubbs Terrace and the surrounding streets are standard bond cleans. Split-system AC filters, balconies, open-plan kitchens, and tiled floors are the norm. The REIWA full checklist applies for both property types.

What Gets Missed

Based on Shenton Park exit reports, common missed areas include:

  • Inside overhead cupboards and pantry shelves
  • Behind the toilet and base of toilet fittings
  • Exhaust fan covers in bathroom and laundry
  • Window tracks in all rooms, including rarely used rooms
  • Light fittings — particularly in spare rooms or studies
