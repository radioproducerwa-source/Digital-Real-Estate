Bond Cleaning Dianella Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Dianella is a large northern suburb roughly 8 kilometres from the Perth CBD. It has a well-established feel, with most of the housing stock dating from the 1960s to the 1980s. Family homes are the dominant property type, with a mix of 3 and 4-bedroom brick homes. There are also a number of duplex units and villa complexes scattered throughout the suburb.

Typical Property Features

Dianella rentals are mostly older-style family homes with features that need specific attention at exit:

  • Ducted evaporative air conditioning — system vents need dusting; pads should be inspected
  • Older-style bathrooms — wall and floor tiles with narrow grout lines that collect mildew
  • Carpeted bedrooms and living rooms — professional steam cleaning typically required
  • Established gardens and rear patios — areas must be swept and free of debris
  • Double lock-up garage — floor swept and free of oil stains; shelving and walls wiped

The Inspection Process

Property managers in Dianella use the REIWA Property Condition Report, comparing the property's current state against the ingoing report. Every room is inspected — kitchen, bathrooms, laundry, all bedrooms, living areas, and outdoor areas. The condition report photographs from move-in are compared to move-out, so the cleaner the ingoing report was, the more your exit clean needs to match it.

Common Issues at Exit

  • Oven — a standard fail point; racks, base, and door glass must all be grease-free
  • Rangehood — underside grease and clogged filters
  • Bathrooms — watermarks on mirrors, soap scum on screens, and dirty grout
  • Carpet — stains, pet hair, or general soiling will result in a deduction
  • Rear garden area — leaf debris, weeds, and general garden waste
