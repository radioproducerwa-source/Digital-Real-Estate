Bond Cleaning Claremont Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Claremont sits on the banks of the Swan River, roughly 9 kilometres west of the CBD. It's an affluent suburb with a strong rental market — a mix of large character homes on generous blocks, prestige riverside apartments, and properties close to Claremont Quarter shopping centre. Bond cleaning standards in Claremont tend to be high, reflecting the premium nature of the rental stock.

What Claremont Properties Look Like

Claremont's rental stock divides broadly into three categories:

  • Large character homes (pre-1960) — high ceilings, jarrah floors, large kitchens, multiple bathrooms
  • Prestige apartments — often newer builds with stone benchtops, quality appliances, and multiple balconies
  • Townhouses and villas — typically 2–3 bedrooms with private courtyards or small gardens

Each type requires a thorough bond clean, and property managers in this area are experienced at identifying shortcuts.

High-Expectation Areas

In Claremont rentals, the following areas receive the most scrutiny at exit inspection:

  • Kitchen — stone or marble benchtops need specific care; ovens and rangehoods must be spotless
  • Bathrooms — frameless shower screens require streak-free glass cleaning
  • Hardwood floors — any residue, streaking, or product marks will be flagged
  • Walls — scuff marks and minor marks should be addressed before the inspection
  • Outdoor areas — large entertaining areas or garden patios must be swept and cleared of debris

Carpet Steam Cleaning

Many Claremont homes have carpet in bedrooms. If carpets were present at the start of your tenancy, professional steam cleaning is almost always required by the lease — a hire machine or DIY clean is unlikely to meet the standard expected in this suburb.
