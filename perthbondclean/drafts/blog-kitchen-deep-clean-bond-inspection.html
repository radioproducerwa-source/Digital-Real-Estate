Kitchen Deep Clean for Bond Inspection Perth

Published 2026  |  5 min read

The kitchen is the most critical room in any Perth bond clean. Property managers know that kitchens accumulate the most grease, residue, and staining over a tenancy — and they inspect them closely. Getting the kitchen right is essential to passing your exit inspection and getting your bond back in full.

The Oven — Your First Priority

The oven is the single most common source of bond deductions across all Perth properties. Carbon and grease build-up on racks, the base, the walls, and the door glass cannot be removed with standard spray-and-wipe products. A proper oven clean requires:

  • Commercial oven cleaner applied generously to all internal surfaces
  • Allow to dwell for the full recommended time — overnight for heavy build-up
  • Remove racks and clean separately in a soaking solution
  • Clean the door glass both interior and exterior
  • Wipe down the oven exterior, handle, and control panel
  • Check inside the drawer below the oven (often overlooked)

If the oven has heavy carbon build-up from years of use, a professional oven clean is the only reliable option.

Rangehood

Rangehood cleaning is the second most common kitchen deduction:

  • Remove and soak the filter in hot water with degreaser for 30 minutes
  • Scrub the filter and rinse thoroughly
  • Clean the underside of the rangehood — grease splatter on the surface and around the burner area
  • Wipe the exterior, buttons, and surrounding cabinetry

Benchtops

Benchtop cleaning depends on the material:

  • Laminate — general purpose cleaner; avoid abrasives which scratch the surface
  • Stone or engineered stone — use a stone-specific cleaner only; never vinegar or bleach (they damage the sealer)
  • Stainless steel — use a stainless steel cleaner; clean in the direction of the grain

Cupboards — Inside and Out

Property managers open every cupboard door and check inside. Empty all cupboards before cleaning. Wipe the interior of every shelf, every drawer, and the back corners. Clean the exterior faces, handles, and the gaps between cupboard doors. Pay particular attention to the cupboard directly below the sink — it often has moisture staining, rust spots, and product residue.

Splashback

Tile splashbacks need grout cleaning as well as tile surface cleaning. Glass splashbacks require a streak-free clean — fingerprints and oil spots show clearly. Painted splashbacks can usually be wiped with a damp cloth and mild detergent.

Sink and Tapware

The kitchen sink should be clean, streak-free, and free of limescale. Tapware should be polished — watermarks on chrome fittings are a common minor deduction. Use a descaling product for limescale around the tap base and around the plughole.

Appliance Exteriors

Wipe the exterior of all appliances left at the property — dishwasher, microwave, fridge (if provided). Clean the door seals and the area between the fridge and adjacent cabinetry. Check behind the fridge if it can be moved — food debris and dust under and behind fridges are commonly missed.
