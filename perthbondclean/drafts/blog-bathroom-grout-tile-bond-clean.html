Bathroom Grout & Tile Bond Clean Perth

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Bathroom grout and tile cleaning is consistently one of the most labour-intensive and closely inspected areas of any Perth bond clean. Grout lines discolour over time from soap scum, hard water deposits, and mildew — and no amount of surface wiping will fix discoloured grout. Here's how to approach this area properly.

Why Grout Is a Bond Problem

Grout is porous. Over time, it absorbs soap residue, mineral deposits from hard water, and mildew — particularly in bathrooms with poor ventilation. This creates discolouration that looks "dirty" even after a surface clean. Property managers distinguish between old, stained grout and grout that hasn't been cleaned — and they expect clean grout at exit regardless of how old the tiles are.

What Actually Works on Grout

Surface wiping doesn't penetrate grout. For effective grout cleaning:

  • Oxygen bleach (like Napisan powder made into a paste) applied to grout lines and left for 20–30 minutes
  • A stiff grout brush (toothbrush-width) to scrub lines systematically
  • A rinse with clean water followed by a wipe with a dry cloth
  • For stubborn mildew stains — commercial grout cleaner with specific anti-mould actives

Avoid chlorine bleach on coloured grout (it fades the colour) and acidic products on natural stone tiles.

Shower Screen Cleaning

Frameless and semi-frameless shower screens are a major inspection point. Hard water deposits and soap scum create a milky film that's visible in any light. To clean shower screens effectively:

  • Use a dedicated shower screen cleaner or neat white vinegar (leave 10 minutes)
  • Use a rubber squeegee on large panes; a microfibre cloth for edges and frames
  • Clean metal tracks and seals with an old toothbrush
  • Check from multiple angles in good light — water marks show at low angles

Floor Tiles — Grout Lines Are Critical

Floor tile grout is often more stained than wall grout due to foot traffic and water pooling. The same oxygen bleach approach works — apply the paste, leave it, scrub, and rinse. For heavily stained floor grout, a steam cleaner (if available) significantly improves the result. Note: very old, brittle grout should not be aggressively scrubbed — if it crumbles under normal cleaning, that's a maintenance issue the landlord should address.

When Grout Cannot Be Restored

Some discolouration — particularly from years of mildew growth in poorly ventilated bathrooms — cannot be reversed with cleaning alone. In these cases, be upfront with the property manager and provide evidence of thorough cleaning efforts. If the discolouration was documented at move-in, it cannot be claimed as tenant damage. If it was not documented, this is why photographing the property on day one is so important.

What Professional Bond Cleaners Do Differently

Professional bond cleaners use commercial-grade grout cleaners, steam equipment, and have the experience to distinguish between recoverable and unrecoverable discolouration. They also know what property managers look for — and they clean accordingly. For bathroom grout, professional cleaning almost always achieves a better result than DIY.
