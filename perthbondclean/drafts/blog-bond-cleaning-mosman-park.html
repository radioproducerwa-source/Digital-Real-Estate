Bond Cleaning Mosman Park Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Mosman Park is one of Perth's most prestigious coastal suburbs, positioned between the Swan River and the Indian Ocean, about 12 kilometres from the CBD. Large homes on generous blocks are the norm, and the rental market attracts executive tenants and families. Property managers in Mosman Park are thorough, and exit inspections reflect the premium nature of the properties.

Property Characteristics

Most Mosman Park rentals are substantial — 3, 4, or 5-bedroom homes, often with large outdoor entertaining areas, double garages, and multiple bathrooms. Some properties include:

  • Swimming pools — pool surrounds need pressure washing; pool itself is separate
  • Large alfresco areas — often include outdoor kitchens or BBQ areas requiring degreasing
  • Multiple living zones — each area must be fully cleaned, including light fittings
  • Double garages — floor must be swept and degreased; any oil stains addressed
  • Guest bathrooms — often underused but still inspected to the same standard

Salt Air and Coastal Considerations

Properties near the coast accumulate salt deposits on windows and fly screens. Window frames and tracks in Mosman Park often require extra attention due to salt residue. Fly screens should be removed and washed separately. Balcony or deck furniture is not part of a standard bond clean, but the surface beneath must be cleaned.

High-Risk Areas at Inspection

Based on Mosman Park exit inspections, the most common deduction triggers are:

  • Oven and outdoor BBQ/kitchen — heavy build-up on larger cooking surfaces
  • Shower screens — frameless glass requires a proper streak-free clean
  • Window tracks on ocean-facing windows — salt and grit accumulation
  • Garage floor — oil drips and tyre marks must be removed
  • Air conditioning filters — larger homes may have multiple split systems
