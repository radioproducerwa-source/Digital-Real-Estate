Bond Cleaning Kensington Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Kensington is one of Perth's better-kept secrets — an inner-south suburb just 4 kilometres from the CBD with tree-lined streets, easy freeway access, and a growing café culture. The suburb has a mix of 1920s–1960s character homes, a number of newer apartment developments, and infill townhouses built over the past decade.

Character Home Cleans

Kensington's older character homes require careful attention at exit:

  • Timber floors — jarrah or pine boards need wood-safe cleaning products
  • High cornices and ceilings — cobwebs and dust accumulate in corners
  • Older kitchen fittings — older ovens, exposed plumbing, and older grouting
  • Leadlight or louvre windows — tracks and frames need careful attention
  • Sash windows — channels often hold layers of grime from years of use

Modern Apartments and Townhouses

Newer developments in Kensington are standard bond cleans — split-system AC, open plan living, tiled floors, and balconies. The standard REIWA checklist applies. AC filter cleaning and balcony cleaning are always included.

What Property Managers Prioritise

In Kensington exits, the areas most commonly flagged at inspection are:

  • Oven and rangehood — grease and carbon build-up
  • Bathroom — grout lines and shower screens
  • Timber floors — streaking or residue from incorrect cleaning products
  • Walls — marks near light switches and door frames
  • Skirting boards — dust accumulation along all wall bases
