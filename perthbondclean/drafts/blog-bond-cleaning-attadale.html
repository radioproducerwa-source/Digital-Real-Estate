Bond Cleaning Attadale Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Attadale is a leafy, established suburb in Perth's southern riverside corridor, about 12 kilometres from the CBD. It's popular with families for its proximity to the river, quality schools, and relaxed suburban feel. Most of the rental stock consists of solid brick homes built from the 1960s to the 1990s, with a growing number of newer townhouses and knock-down rebuilds.

Typical Attadale Properties

Attadale rentals are predominantly 3–4 bedroom family homes with:

  • Brick and tile construction — walls and surfaces are durable but must be clean
  • Established gardens and alfresco areas — outdoor entertaining spaces must be swept and clear
  • Double garages — floor degreasing may be required if oil stains are present
  • Older bathrooms — grout lines in older tiled bathrooms require specific attention
  • Laundry rooms — trough, shelving, and appliance surfaces all checked at inspection

What Property Managers Check

Exit inspections in Attadale follow the REIWA Property Condition Report (PCR) standard. In this suburb, the most commonly failed areas are:

  • Oven — carbon build-up on racks and base, grease on door glass
  • Rangehood — filter grease and splattered oil on the underside
  • Bathroom grout — mildew or discolouration in older grout lines
  • Rear of kitchen — behind freestanding appliances if accessible
  • Window screens — need to be removed, washed, and dried before reinstallation

Carpet and Floor Cleaning

Many Attadale family homes have carpet in bedrooms and living areas. Professional steam cleaning is almost always required by the lease and must be completed by an IICRC-certified operator to meet the standard expected at inspection. Keep the receipt — property managers will ask for it.
