Bond Cleaning in Perth Winter — Tips & Advice

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Perth's winter (June–August) is mild by most Australian standards, but it brings challenges that directly affect bond cleaning: rain, humidity, slower drying times, and the need to work around wet weather windows. Here's how to approach a winter exit clean successfully.

Drying Times Are Longer

In Perth's winter humidity, surfaces take longer to dry after cleaning. This affects:

  • Mopped floors — tiles and vinyl stay wet longer; allow adequate drying time before walking on them
  • Fly screens — must be fully dry before reinstallation; damp screens will show streaks
  • Carpet steam cleaning — carpets take longer to dry in winter; allow 4–6 hours (not 2–3 as in summer)
  • Oven after cleaning — leave the door ajar after cleaning to allow the interior to dry
  • Shower screens — buff with a dry microfibre cloth after cleaning to avoid watermarks

Windows and Outdoor Areas in the Rain

Window cleaning in rain is challenging — raindrops will mark interior glass if windows are open, and exterior windows will become dirty again immediately if it's raining. Schedule window cleaning for a dry day or dry period, even if this means cleaning them separately from the main bond clean. Interior window cleaning can always proceed regardless of the weather.

Managing Mould in Winter

Perth winters see an increase in condensation-related mould, particularly in bathrooms, laundries, and on window frames. Mould that has appeared during winter at the end of your tenancy needs to be addressed before exit. Use a mould-specific spray (not standard surface cleaner) and treat the area with ventilation. If mould recurred regularly despite your best efforts, note this in writing — chronic mould in poorly ventilated properties may be a maintenance issue rather than a cleaning failure.

Heater and Fireplace Cleaning

Winter is when heaters and fireplaces are used. Gas heater filters and fireplace surrounds should be cleaned at exit. Soot staining around fireplace openings should be addressed, and glass fireplace doors should be cleaned with a specialist glass cleaner. Gas heater vents accumulate dust over winter use — vacuum and wipe the filter cover.

Timing Your Bond Clean

Book your bond clean for a window of 2–3 dry days if possible. This allows the clean to proceed without rain interference on outdoor areas and windows, and gives adequate drying time for carpets and floors before the exit inspection. Many Perth bond cleaners are flexible about scheduling around weather forecasts — ask when you book.

Winter Inspection Specifics

Property managers in winter will look for mould (particularly in bathrooms and on window frames), condensation marks on windows, and any salt or dirt tracked in from winter rain. The property should be presented as clean as it would be in any other season — winter doesn't lower the standard expected at exit.
