Bond Cleaning Canning Vale Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Canning Vale is a large, family-oriented suburb about 20 kilometres south of the Perth CBD. It's a diverse suburb with several distinct estates, each developed at different times — meaning the rental stock ranges from 1990s brick homes to newer 2010s builds with modern finishes. The suburb has a large, active rental market and experienced property managers.

Newer Builds vs Established Homes

Canning Vale's property mix creates two distinct cleaning scenarios:

  • Newer homes (2010s+) — stone benchtops, quality appliances, alfresco areas, and double garages
  • Established homes (1990s–2000s) — older fixtures, carpets throughout, and more traditional layouts

Newer properties are held to a higher visual standard at exit because the ingoing condition was pristine. Any mark, stain, or missed area is immediately visible against a near-new finish.

Large Properties — What Takes Longer

Canning Vale's homes are generally larger than inner-suburban properties. This means:

  • Multiple bathrooms — each must be fully cleaned to the same standard
  • Large kitchens — more bench space, more appliances, larger ovens
  • Alfresco areas and patios — must be swept, washed, and free of debris
  • Double garages — floor, walls, and any built-in shelving
  • Study or theatre rooms — often forgotten but fully inspected

Common Inspection Issues

  • Oven and rangehood — especially in family homes with heavy use
  • Carpet — professionally steam cleaned with receipt
  • Alfresco area — BBQ residue, general grime, and leaf debris
  • Garage floor — oil stains and tyre marks
  • Window screens — often missed; must be removed and washed
