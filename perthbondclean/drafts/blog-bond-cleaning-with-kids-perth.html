Bond Cleaning With Kids Still in the House Perth

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Bond cleaning with young children in the house is one of the most challenging rental exits a family can face. You need to clean rooms to an inspection-standard while little hands are undoing your work behind you. Here are the strategies that actually work.

Plan Room-by-Room, Not All at Once

Trying to clean the entire property at once with kids present is inefficient and frustrating. A better approach:

  • Tackle one room at a time and keep children out of completed rooms
  • Start with the rooms your children use least (spare room, study, formal living)
  • Leave the children's rooms until last and arrange them to stay elsewhere for their final night if possible
  • Clean the kitchen and bathrooms when you can arrange childcare or when children are asleep

Use a Professional Bond Cleaner

With children in the house, professional cleaning is almost always the right call — not just for convenience but for quality. A bond cleaner will complete the work faster, more thoroughly, and to a standard that a parent juggling supervision cannot match. Most Perth bond cleaners will work around your family's schedule and can be booked for early morning or weekend slots.

Specific Challenges with Kids

Children create specific cleaning issues beyond general tidiness:

  • Crayon and texta marks on walls — address with a magic eraser or methylated spirits before the cleaner arrives
  • Carpet stains — food, juice, and craft materials; treat stains as soon as they occur, not at exit
  • Handprints on walls, doors, and light switches — check at child height throughout the property
  • Stickers on surfaces — warm the sticker with a hairdryer to soften the adhesive before removing
  • Growth charts drawn on walls — these often require repainting, which needs landlord permission

Timing Your Exit

If possible, move your children out to grandparents or friends for the final 24–48 hours. This allows the cleaner to work uninterrupted and allows you to maintain the clean standard until the inspection. Even if you can't move your children out, having them out of the house for the inspection itself is strongly advisable.

Children's Furniture and Fittings

Remove all wall-mounted items — picture hooks, growth charts, command strips, and adhesive wall decals — before the cleaner arrives. Patch any holes (with landlord permission for sizeable holes). Command strips and adhesive decals often leave residue that needs to be addressed separately.

Pet Issues

Families with pets face additional obligations. If your lease required flea treatment at exit, ensure this is arranged before the exit inspection, and provide the receipt. Carpet with pet hair will require professional steam cleaning regardless of its visual appearance.
