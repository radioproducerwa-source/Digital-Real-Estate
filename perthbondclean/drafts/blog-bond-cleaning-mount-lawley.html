Bond Cleaning Mount Lawley Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Mount Lawley is consistently one of Perth's most popular rental suburbs, combining proximity to the CBD with a laid-back café and arts culture. The property mix is predominantly older — Federation, inter-war, and post-war character homes — alongside converted character apartments and a number of newer developments near the train station.

Character Homes — Extra Attention Required

The heritage-style homes that define Mount Lawley come with cleaning requirements you won't find in a modern build:

  • Jarrah floorboards — require careful cleaning; avoid excess moisture and harsh solvents
  • Leadlight or stained-glass windows — clean frame and sill only; do not use abrasive products on glass
  • High ceilings and ornate cornices — cobwebs and dust build up in corners and crevices
  • Older kitchen layouts — often have exposed plumbing under sinks that collects grime
  • Sash windows — tracks and sash channels accumulate years of paint chips and dust

Apartment Rentals in Mount Lawley

Converted character apartments (often in 1930s–1950s blocks) present their own challenges: shared laundries, older tile work, and bathrooms with pedestal basins. Modern apartments near Beaufort Street are straightforward — standard split-system AC, open-plan kitchen, and a balcony. For both, the REIWA standard checklist applies in full.

Most Common Deductions in Mount Lawley

Based on exit inspection reports, the most common deductions in Mount Lawley properties are:

  • Oven cleaning — grease and carbon build-up on racks, base, and door glass
  • Bathroom tile grout — discolouration and mildew in older grout lines
  • Floorboard cleaning — product residue or streaking left after mopping
  • Ceiling cobwebs — particularly in older homes with high ceilings
  • AC filter cleaning — split-system filters must be washed and dried
