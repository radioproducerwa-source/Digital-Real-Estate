Bond Cleaning St James Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

St James is an affordable, well-located suburb about 6 kilometres from the Perth CBD and close to Curtin University. The rental market is active, with a high proportion of students, young professionals, and share-house tenants. Property types include older brick homes, villa units, and a mix of 1-bedroom and 2-bedroom apartments. Due to the high rental turnover, property managers in St James are experienced and methodical.

Share Houses — Extra Considerations

Share houses have specific bond cleaning challenges that single-tenant properties don't:

  • Multiple occupants mean accumulated grease, grime, and general wear across all areas
  • Shared bathrooms are often the most problematic — multiple users, minimal maintenance
  • Communal kitchens accumulate more oven and stovetop grease than single-occupant properties
  • Separate bonds may be held for each tenant — coordinate the exit clean to avoid disputes between tenants
  • Carpet in shared living areas sees heavy use and requires professional steam cleaning

Older Unit Complexes

Many St James rentals are 1960s–1970s villa or duplex units with older fixtures. Bathroom tiles, older caulking, and dated kitchen fittings require careful attention. Condition issues that pre-dated your tenancy should have been documented in the ingoing PCR — if they weren't, photograph them clearly before you exit.

Most Common Deductions

  • Oven — the single most common deduction across all Perth properties
  • Bathrooms — soap scum, mildew in grout, and dirty exhaust fans
  • Carpet — especially in shared living areas with high foot traffic
  • Walls — marks from furniture, posters, and general living
  • Window tracks — grit and insect debris
