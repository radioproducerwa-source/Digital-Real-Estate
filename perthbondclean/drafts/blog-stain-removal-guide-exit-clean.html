Stain Removal Guide Before Your Exit Clean Perth

Published 2026  |  5 min read

Stains are one of the most common causes of bond deductions in Perth. While a professional bond cleaner will handle routine grime and grease, stubborn stains — particularly on carpets, walls, and hard surfaces — may require pre-treatment or specialist products. Knowing which stains can be tackled and which need professional help can save you significant money.

Carpet Stains

Carpet stains are the biggest risk area. The key rule: act on stains when they happen, not at exit. Old set stains are much harder (and sometimes impossible) to remove. For exit cleaning purposes:

  • Red wine or berry stains — treat with cold water and a small amount of dishwashing liquid; never hot water (sets the stain)
  • Coffee and tea — blot immediately with cold water; use white vinegar solution after
  • Pet urine — enzyme-based cleaners are the only effective option; standard cleaners mask the smell temporarily
  • Grease or oil — apply cornflour or baking soda first to absorb the oil, then clean
  • Old, set stains — professional steam cleaning is required; some may not come out regardless

Wall Stains and Marks

Scuff marks and minor stains on walls can often be addressed before the exit clean:

  • Light scuffs and pencil marks — a magic eraser (melamine foam) works well on most painted surfaces
  • Crayon marks — use white vinegar or a small amount of WD-40, then clean with soapy water
  • Blu-tack or poster residue — warm the residue with a hairdryer, then roll it off; clean the mark with methylated spirits
  • Grease marks near the kitchen — degrease with a small amount of dish soap and warm water

Caution: Do not try to repaint over marks without the landlord's written permission and matching paint — a visible patch is worse than the original mark.

Bathroom and Tile Stains

Hard water stains, soap scum, and rust stains in bathrooms are common in Perth's hard water areas:

  • Hard water / limescale — white vinegar or citric acid solution; leave for 15 minutes before scrubbing
  • Rust stains — Bar Keepers Friend or a dedicated rust remover; avoid abrasives on acrylic surfaces
  • Soap scum on screens — dedicated shower screen cleaner or straight white vinegar
  • Grout discolouration — oxygen bleach paste (like Napisan) applied with an old toothbrush

Kitchen Stains

  • Baked-on oven grease — commercial oven cleaner (allow overnight dwell time)
  • Benchtop stains (laminate) — baking soda paste; avoid bleach on laminate
  • Stone benchtop stains — specific stone cleaner only; vinegar and bleach damage stone sealers
  • Rangehood grease — degreaser soaked into the filter for 30 minutes, then scrubbed

When to Call a Professional

Some stains genuinely cannot be removed by DIY methods — old carpet stains that have set and dried, severe grout discolouration, or stains on specialty materials. In these cases, have a professional assess the situation before the exit inspection. Knowing in advance saves you from a deduction surprise.
