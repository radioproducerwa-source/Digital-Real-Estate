Bond Cleaning Applecross Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Applecross sits on the southern bank of the Swan River, about 10 kilometres from the Perth CBD. It's a premium suburb with excellent schools and river access, attracting families and professionals. The rental stock is a mix of large character homes, executive townhouses, and newer apartment developments along Canning Highway.

What Makes Applecross Bond Cleans Different

Applecross properties tend to be larger and better appointed than average Perth rentals. This means:

  • More rooms to clean — many properties have 4+ bedrooms and multiple living areas
  • Premium appliances — larger ovens, integrated dishwashers, and quality rangehoods
  • Stone or engineered stone benchtops — require appropriate non-acidic cleaners
  • Timber or hybrid floors — cannot be cleaned with standard mop and bucket
  • Larger bathrooms — often with double vanities, spa baths, or large walk-in showers

Common Deductions in Applecross

Property managers in Applecross apply the REIWA standard but with the expectation that premium finishes are returned in premium condition. Watch out for:

  • Kitchen — inside all cupboards and drawers, not just the oven and rangehood
  • Bathrooms — grout, exhaust fans, shower screens, and behind the toilet
  • Light fittings — pendant lights and spotlights collect dust and insects
  • Sliding door tracks — heavy foot traffic means significant grit build-up
  • Garage or carport — swept floor, clean walls, and cleared storage areas

Carpet vs Hard Floors

Applecross rentals increasingly feature timber or hybrid flooring throughout. If the property has carpet in any rooms, professional steam cleaning is typically required by lease. For timber floors, a professional clean using appropriate products is recommended to avoid streaking or product residue that may be flagged at inspection.
