Window Cleaning for Bond Inspection Perth

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Windows are one of the most visible parts of any property — and one of the areas property managers scrutinise closely at the exit inspection. Understanding what "window cleaning" means in a bond cleaning context will help you avoid unexpected deductions.

What's Included in a Standard Bond Clean

A standard Perth bond clean includes the following window-related tasks:

  • Window sills — cleaned and free of dust, dirt, insect debris, and staining
  • Window tracks — one of the most commonly failed areas; must be clear of grit, debris, and dead insects
  • Window frames — wiped inside and out (accessible without ladders)
  • Interior window glass — cleaned streak-free on the inside
  • Fly screens — removed, washed, dried, and replaced

What's NOT in a Standard Bond Clean (Extra Cost)

External window cleaning and high-access windows require specialist window cleaners and are not part of a standard bond clean price:

  • External glass above ground floor — requires ladders or a squeegee pole
  • Second-storey windows — specialist access and insurance required
  • Louvre window blades — specialist cleaning due to multiple individual glass panels
  • Skylights — roof access required; quoted separately

If your lease specifies that windows must be returned to the same condition as at move-in and the ingoing report showed clean external windows, you may be obligated to arrange external window cleaning even if it's not included in a standard bond clean.

Fly Screens — A Commonly Missed Step

Fly screens are part of every window clean and are regularly missed by DIY bond cleaners. They must be:

  • Removed from the frame
  • Washed with mild detergent and warm water
  • Rinsed and allowed to dry fully before replacement
  • Reinstalled correctly in their frames

Fly screens left in place and wiped (rather than removed and washed) will still show dust and grime at close inspection.

Perth's Dusty Environment

Perth's dry, sandy climate means window tracks and sills accumulate more grime than in most other Australian cities. Properties facing east or near major roads are particularly susceptible. After windy periods or dust storms (common in summer), tracks can fill with a surprising amount of fine sand — plan to address these on exit regardless of how recently you last cleaned them.

Tips for a Streak-Free Interior Glass Clean

To achieve a streak-free glass clean that will pass inspection: use a window cleaner spray with a rubber squeegee on larger panes; use a lint-free microfibre cloth on smaller windows and frames; clean in overlapping vertical strokes; and clean in overcast conditions where possible (direct sunlight dries the cleaner too fast, causing streaks).
