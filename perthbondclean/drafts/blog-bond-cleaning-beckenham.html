Bond Cleaning Beckenham Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Beckenham is an affordable, well-located suburb in Perth's south-east, about 14 kilometres from the CBD near the Cannington area. It has a large rental population and an active market, with most properties being older 3-bedroom brick homes. The suburb's affordability makes it popular with families and working renters looking for space at a reasonable price.

Older Home Characteristics

Beckenham homes are predominantly 1970s and 1980s construction with features common to the era:

  • Carpeted living areas and bedrooms — professional steam cleaning required by most leases
  • Older bathrooms — bathroom tiles with wider grout lines that discolour over time
  • Freestanding or under-bench ovens — often older models that accumulate heavy grease
  • Evaporative or ducted cooling — system must be cleaned at exit
  • Rear garden areas and patios — swept and free of debris at exit

What the Exit Inspection Covers

The REIWA exit inspection in WA covers every area of the property — not just the obvious ones. Commonly missed areas in older Beckenham homes include:

  • Inside all kitchen cupboards and drawers — including the back corners
  • Under the kitchen sink — grime, moisture stains, and product residue
  • Laundry room — trough, shelving, and applicance surfaces
  • Exhaust fans in bathrooms and laundry — often clogged with lint and dust
  • Rear of toilet — base and surrounding tiles

Common Deductions

  • Carpet — stains or failure to steam clean professionally
  • Oven — the universal fail point across Perth rentals
  • Bathroom grout — discolouration and mildew
  • Garden — overgrown or debris-laden
  • Walls — marks and scuffs near high-traffic areas
