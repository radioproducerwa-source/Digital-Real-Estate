End of Lease Carpet Cleaning Requirements Perth

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Carpet cleaning at the end of a tenancy is one of the most frequently disputed areas in Perth bond claims. Understanding what the law says, what your lease likely requires, and what actually meets the standard will help you protect your bond.

What WA Law Says

The Residential Tenancies Act 1987 (WA) does not specifically mandate professional carpet cleaning — it requires that the property be returned in the same condition as at the start of the tenancy, allowing for fair wear and tear. If the carpets were professionally cleaned at the start of your tenancy (documented in the ingoing PCR), the same standard is expected at exit. If they weren't noted as professionally cleaned at move-in, you cannot be required to do more than return them to the same condition.

What Your Lease Clause May Say

Many Perth leases include a specific carpet cleaning clause requiring professional steam cleaning at exit. If your lease includes such a clause, you are contractually obligated to arrange professional cleaning regardless of the carpet's actual condition. Always check your lease and keep the receipt — property managers will ask for it.

Professional Steam Cleaning vs DIY

The standard required by most Perth leases and property managers is professional steam cleaning (hot water extraction) by a qualified operator — not a hire machine from Bunnings or a dry clean. Reasons this matters:

  • Hire machines have significantly lower heat and extraction power — they don't achieve the same result
  • Property managers have experience with the difference — a hire machine clean is often visually distinguishable
  • Most bond guarantees are conditional on professional steam cleaning being completed
  • If a dispute arises, having an invoice from a certified operator is strong evidence

If You Have Pets

If you've had pets during the tenancy, most leases require flea treatment in addition to steam cleaning. This must be completed by a licensed pest controller. Keep both receipts — one for carpet cleaning, one for pest control. Some Perth leases require this even if no fleas were present during the tenancy.

Carpet Stains and Deductions

Professional steam cleaning will remove most general soiling and light staining. It will not remove all set stains, particularly old liquid stains, pet urine (odour may remain), or bleach damage. If your carpet has stains that may not come out, get an assessment before the exit inspection — a professional cleaner can advise whether the stain is removable or will result in a deduction regardless. Knowing in advance allows you to budget or negotiate a settlement.

Choosing a Carpet Steam Cleaner

Use an IICRC-certified operator where possible, ensure they provide an invoice suitable for bond purposes, and check they carry public liability insurance. Many bond cleaners can arrange carpet steam cleaning as part of a combined package — ask when you quote your bond clean.
