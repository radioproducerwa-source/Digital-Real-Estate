Bond Cleaning Bull Creek Perth — Renter's Guide

Published 2026  |  4 min read

Bull Creek is a family-oriented suburb in Perth's south, situated about 14 kilometres from the CBD near Murdoch University and Westfield Booragoon. The rental stock is predominantly 1970s and 1980s brick homes — solid, spacious, and well-established. The suburb is quiet and residential, attracting families and university-affiliated professionals.

Typical Bull Creek Rentals

Most Bull Creek rentals are 3–4 bedroom brick homes with:

  • Carpet throughout living areas and bedrooms — professional steam cleaning typically required
  • Older-style kitchens with laminate benchtops and freestanding or under-bench ovens
  • Evaporative air conditioning — a common feature in homes of this era
  • Large backyards with patios — outdoor areas must be swept and cleared at exit
  • Double carports or garages — floor must be swept and free of debris

Age of Property Considerations

1970s and 1980s homes have specific cleaning characteristics. Tiles throughout bathrooms and kitchens often have wide grout lines that accumulate staining. The older caulking around baths and showers may discolour — if it was discoloured at move-in, it should be documented in the ingoing PCR. Ceiling fans and pendant lights are common in homes of this era and need to be cleaned.

Common Deductions

  • Carpet — pet hair, stains, or failure to professionally steam clean
  • Oven and rangehood — heavy grease and carbon from family cooking
  • Bathroom — soap scum, mirror watermarks, and discoloured grout
  • Garden areas — overgrown grass, leaf debris, or general garden waste
  • Evaporative cooler — pads not cleaned or condition not noted at ingoing
