How to Choose a Bond Cleaner in Perth (Without Getting Burnt)

Published May 2025  |  5 min read

With dozens of bond cleaning companies operating across Perth — from one-person operations to larger franchises — choosing the right one isn't as simple as finding the cheapest quote. A bad choice can cost you far more than you saved. Here's exactly how to evaluate your options.

The 5 Questions to Ask Before Booking

1. Do you offer a bond back guarantee?

This is non-negotiable. A bond back guarantee means the cleaner will return and re-clean any areas flagged by your property manager within a set time window (usually 48–72 hours) at no extra cost. Without this, you carry all the risk. Any reputable Perth bond cleaner offers this as standard — if they don't, keep looking.

2. Are your cleaners insured and police-checked?

You're granting access to your property — often without being present. Public liability insurance protects you if something is damaged. Police clearances are standard practice for any legitimate cleaning company operating in Perth. Ask directly and expect a clear yes.

3. Do you use the REIWA Property Condition Report checklist?

Perth property managers use the REIWA checklist. Your cleaner should be working from the same document — otherwise there's no way to guarantee they've covered what your property manager will check. A cleaner who gives you a vague "we clean everything" answer rather than confirming they follow the REIWA checklist is a risk.

4. Is your quote flat-rate or hourly?

Hourly quotes that balloon on the day are a common complaint. Get a flat-rate quote based on your number of bedrooms and bathrooms, with any add-ons (carpet, garage, oven) itemised separately. Make sure you have written confirmation before the clean starts.

5. Do you have verifiable reviews?

Google Reviews, Hipages, or Oneflare are the most reliable sources. Look for recent reviews (within the last 6 months) that mention specific details — the bond was returned, the property manager was satisfied, the oven was properly cleaned. Generic 5-star reviews with no detail are less useful.

Red Flags to Walk Away From

  • No ABN or registered business name
  • Quote given without asking property size, condition, or add-ons
  • Unusually low price with no explanation (under $150 for any property)
  • Cash only with no invoice or receipt offered
  • No guarantee, or a guarantee with so many exclusions it's meaningless
  • No response to questions about insurance or police checks
  • Pressure to book immediately without time to compare

Price vs Quality: Getting the Balance Right

The cheapest bond clean in Perth is almost never the right bond clean. A quote under $200 for a 2-bedroom property, for example, either involves cutting corners or using products that won't meet inspection standards. Compare 2–3 quotes from reputable, insured cleaners and focus on the guarantee and checklist rather than finding the absolute minimum price.
