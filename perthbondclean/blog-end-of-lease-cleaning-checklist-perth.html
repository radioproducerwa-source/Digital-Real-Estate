End of Lease Cleaning Checklist Perth — Room by Room (REIWA-Aligned)

Published May 2025  |  8 min read  |  Print this checklist

Moving out of a rental in Perth? The exit inspection is the moment of truth — and the REIWA Property Condition Report is the document your property manager will be working from. Miss an item on it and you risk a bond deduction.

This checklist covers every room and area of a standard Perth rental property. Use it to verify your own clean, brief a professional cleaner, or simply understand what will be inspected on the day.

Tip: This checklist is interactive — tick each item as you go. You can also print this page for a physical copy to take to your property.

Kitchen

The kitchen is the most common source of bond deductions in Perth exit inspections. Oven grease and rangehood filters are checked closely.

Oven & Cooking Appliances

  • Oven interior wiped — all grease and baked-on residue removed
  • Oven racks scrubbed clean
  • Oven door glass cleaned inside and out
  • Stovetop burners, drip trays, and knobs cleaned
  • Rangehood filters degreased (soak in hot soapy water or dishwasher)
  • Rangehood exterior wiped down
  • Microwave interior and exterior cleaned

Cupboards, Benches & Sink

  • All cupboard doors wiped inside and out
  • Cupboard shelves wiped — check for crumbs and spills
  • Drawers emptied, wiped inside and out
  • Benchtops scrubbed and dried
  • Splashback degreased and wiped
  • Sink scrubbed, taps polished, drain cleared
  • Dishwasher filter cleaned, interior wiped

General Kitchen

  • Skirting boards behind and under appliances wiped
  • Light switches and power points wiped
  • Window sills, tracks, and glass cleaned
  • Floor swept and mopped — including under fridge space

Bathrooms

Bathrooms are heavily scrutinised. Soap scum, mould on grout, and limescale on taps are the most commonly flagged issues.

Shower

  • Shower screen or curtain scrubbed — no soap scum or water marks
  • Tiles and grout scrubbed — mould treated if present
  • Shower head descaled
  • Shower tray or base scrubbed, drain cleared of hair
  • Shower recess walls wiped top to bottom

Toilet

  • Toilet bowl scrubbed inside (under rim especially)
  • Cistern wiped outside
  • Toilet seat and lid cleaned both sides
  • Base and behind toilet wiped
  • Flush button cleaned

Vanity & Basin

  • Basin scrubbed and dried
  • Taps polished — no limescale
  • Vanity cupboards wiped inside and out
  • Mirror cleaned — no streaks
  • Exhaust fan cover dusted and wiped
  • Floor mopped — including behind the toilet

Laundry

Laundry Area

  • Laundry tub and trough scrubbed
  • Taps cleaned and polished
  • Washing machine lint filter cleaned (if leaving machine)
  • Cupboards and shelving wiped
  • Floor swept and mopped
  • Exhaust fan cover cleaned if present

Bedrooms

Each Bedroom

  • All built-in wardrobe interiors vacuumed and wiped
  • Wardrobe shelves and rails wiped
  • Wardrobe doors (inside and outside) cleaned
  • Skirting boards wiped along all walls
  • Window sills, tracks, and glass cleaned
  • Blinds or curtains dusted — each slat or fold
  • Light switches and power points wiped
  • Ceiling fan blades and light fittings dusted
  • Walls spot cleaned for scuffs and marks
  • Carpets vacuumed thoroughly (professionally steam cleaned if lease requires)

Living & Dining Areas

Living Room / Dining Room

  • All skirting boards wiped
  • All window sills, tracks, and glass cleaned
  • Blinds dusted (each slat — this is commonly missed)
  • Light switches, dimmers, and power points wiped
  • Ceiling fan and light fittings dusted
  • Air conditioning filters removed and cleaned
  • TV aerial connection points wiped
  • Walls spot cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped (or carpets vacuumed)

Garage & Outdoor Areas

Garage

  • Floor swept — remove oil stains if possible
  • Walls wiped where marked or dusty
  • Garage door wiped inside and out
  • Remote controls left (and working)
  • Any shelving wiped

Balcony, Patio & Outdoor

  • Balcony or patio floor swept and hosed down
  • Balcony glass or balustrade wiped
  • BBQ area cleaned if applicable
  • Garden areas tidy — leaves raked, bins emptied
  • Clothesline wiped

Whole-of-Property Final Checks

Final Walkthrough

  • All light globes working — replace any that are blown
  • All keys, remotes, and fobs accounted for
  • All personal belongings removed
  • All rubbish removed — including from bins
  • All walls checked for picture hooks, nails, or damage
  • All doors and windows open and close properly
  • Smoke detector batteries functional
  • Air conditioning units cleaned (filters wiped)
Pro tip: Walk through the property with your ingoing condition report and compare each room. Property managers will do exactly this — so getting ahead of it is the best way to protect your bond.

Common Items That Cause Bond Deductions in Perth

Based on our experience across hundreds of Perth exit cleans, these are the areas most frequently flagged by property managers:

  • Oven grease — the number one cause of bond deductions. Budget extra time here.
  • Shower screen soap scum — requires a dedicated glass cleaner or acid-based solution.
  • Blind slats — each slat needs to be wiped individually. Often skipped by DIYers.
  • Skirting boards — particularly behind furniture where dust accumulates.
  • Rangehood filters — grease builds up invisibly and is always checked.
  • Wardrobe tracks — dust and debris collect in the bottom tracks.
  • Carpet stains — even small ones can trigger a professional cleaning requirement.

Do I Have to Professionally Clean in Western Australia?

The Residential Tenancies Act 1987 (WA) does not require you to hire a professional cleaner — but it does require you to return the property in a "reasonable state of cleanliness" consistent with the ingoing condition. Many lease agreements go further and specifically require professional carpet steam cleaning, and some require professional cleaning throughout.

Check your lease carefully. If carpet steam cleaning is stipulated, you'll need to provide a receipt — and most property managers will ask for one from a licensed operator.
