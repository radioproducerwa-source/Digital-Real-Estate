Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bond cleaning in Perth.

Bond cleaning, also known as end-of-lease cleaning or vacate cleaning, is a deep clean of a rental property when a tenant is moving out. It covers every area of the home — kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas and outdoor spaces — to restore the property to the condition it was in at the start of the tenancy. A thorough bond clean is required by most landlords and property managers in Perth before releasing a tenant's bond.
Bond cleaning prices in Perth vary depending on the size of the property, its condition, and any add-ons like carpet steam cleaning. As a general guide: a 1-bedroom unit starts from around $250–$350, a 2x1 home is $320–$450, a 3x2 is $400–$600, and larger 4-bedroom homes range from $550–$850+. Carpet cleaning is usually quoted separately. Get a free, no-obligation quote from us to get the exact price for your property.
