How Much Does Business Video Cost in Perth?

Pricing depends on the type of video, length, complexity and whether filming is required. Here are indicative ranges to help you plan.

Video Type Indicative Price Range Typical Turnaround
Social Media Reel Edit (footage supplied) $150 – $300 2–3 business days
Short Promo / Brand Video (edit only) $500 – $1,500 3–5 business days
Corporate Video (filming + editing) $1,500 – $5,000 1–2 weeks
Real Estate Property Video $400 – $1,200 2–5 business days
Event Highlight Reel $800 – $2,500 3–7 business days
Full Production (multi-day shoot + edit) $3,000+ 2–4 weeks

These are indicative ranges — get a free quote for your specific project. Final pricing depends on your brief, location, length and creative requirements.
