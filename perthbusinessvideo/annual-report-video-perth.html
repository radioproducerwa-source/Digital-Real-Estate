The traditional printed annual report is giving way to video. For Perth-based companies, councils, nonprofits and associations, an annual report video is a more engaging, accessible and shareable way to communicate the year's achievements to stakeholders, members, donors and the public. A well-produced year-in-review video builds confidence, demonstrates accountability, and creates lasting documentary value for your organisation.

The challenge with the annual report format has always been engagement. A well-designed PDF or printed document communicates the information — but a video version creates an experience. It can use the CEO's voice and face to build personal connection. It can use footage and photography from the year to show rather than tell. It can communicate the scale of what your organisation has achieved in a way that a page of numbers and paragraphs simply can't.

Perth Business Video connects organisations with video professionals who understand the specific requirements of annual report and stakeholder communication content. They understand the balance between transparency and narrative — how to present achievements compellingly without veering into pure marketing. They understand the sensitivity around forward-looking statements for listed entities. And they understand how to work with finance, communications and executive teams to get a final product approved within tight annual reporting timelines.

Whether you're an ASX-listed company, a local council, a nonprofit or a member association, an annual report video is one of the most valuable communications investments you can make each year. It works at AGMs, in email communications to stakeholders, on your website, and as a public accountability document that demonstrates the seriousness with which you take your responsibilities.

Why Perth Organisations Choose Annual Report Video

An annual report video communicates your year's achievements with impact and clarity.

  • ▶More engaging than printed reports — A 3-6 minute video is watched from start to finish far more often than a 40-page PDF is read — significantly improving stakeholder comprehension of your year's story.
  • ▶Accessible and shareable with all stakeholders — Video can be shared via email, embedded on your website, distributed on LinkedIn and shown at events — reaching stakeholders wherever they are.
  • ▶Works for AGMs, websites and email communications — A single well-produced annual report video works across multiple stakeholder touchpoints — from the AGM screen to the investor relations page of your website.
  • ▶Creates a lasting record of organisational milestones — Year-in-review videos create a documentary archive of your organisation's evolution — valuable for long-term stakeholders, new board members and historical records.
  • ▶Builds confidence with investors, members and donors — A professionally produced annual report video signals that your organisation is well-run, transparent and serious about its communications obligations.
  • ▶Suitable for ASX-listed companies, nonprofits and councils — The format works across entity types — we understand the different communication requirements of listed companies, charities and public sector organisations.
