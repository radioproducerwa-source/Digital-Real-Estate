Perth businesses are fighting for attention on social media. Short-form video is the most powerful tool available — but only when it's edited for the platform, optimised for mobile, and created with the right pacing and style. Our network of social media video editors understands what works on each platform and delivers content built to perform, not just to look nice.

Each social platform has its own rhythm. What works on Instagram Reels doesn't necessarily work on LinkedIn. TikTok rewards authenticity and trending audio. Facebook video ads need a hook within the first two seconds to survive the news feed. A generic edit posted across all channels leaves engagement and reach on the table.

Perth Business Video connects you with editors who live and breathe social media. They understand aspect ratios, optimal video lengths, caption placement, music licensing, and how to structure a video so it holds attention from the first frame to the last. Whether you have raw footage from your phone or professionally shot material, they can shape it into content that performs.

From a single Instagram Reel to an ongoing monthly content package, we can match you with the right editor for your volume, budget and brand. Most social media edits are returned within 24 to 48 hours — so your content stays timely and your posting schedule stays consistent.

Why Perth Businesses Choose Social Media Video Editing

Professionally edited social media video delivers measurably better results than unedited or generic content.

  • ▶Higher organic reach — Platform algorithms favour video content, and well-edited video with strong completion rates gets shown to more people.
  • ▶Platform-specific formatting — Correct aspect ratios, lengths and safe zones for each platform so your content looks intentional, not cobbled together.
  • ▶Fast 24-48hr turnaround — Stay relevant and post consistently without waiting weeks for edited content to come back.
  • ▶Caption and text overlay inclusion — Most social video is watched on mute. Captions and text overlays are essential for reach and accessibility.
  • ▶Music selection and sync — Royalty-free music that fits your brand, properly synced to the edit so it enhances rather than distracts.
  • ▶Consistent brand feel across content — Fonts, colours, transitions and style maintained across all your content so your brand looks cohesive and professional.
