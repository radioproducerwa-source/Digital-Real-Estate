About Perth Mechanic

Perth's trusted service for connecting drivers with qualified, affordable mechanics across every suburb.

Why We Started Perth Mechanic

Perth has over 1.5 million registered vehicles and hundreds of independent mechanic shops — but finding a trustworthy, fairly priced mechanic is still surprisingly difficult for most drivers. The market is fragmented, pricing is opaque, and the average driver has no reliable way to verify a mechanic's credentials before handing over their keys.

Perth Mechanic was built to solve that problem. We've built a curated network of licensed, insured mechanics across the Perth metro area. When you submit a request, we match you with a verified local mechanic who is qualified for your job, available in your area, and committed to transparent pricing.

We're not a workshop. We don't employ mechanics directly. We're a connection service — and that means we have no incentive to recommend anything other than the right mechanic for your situation.

Why Drivers Trust Perth Mechanic

We've built the service around the things Perth drivers told us matter most.

🛠️

Licensed Mechanics Only

Every mechanic in our network holds a current Motor Vehicle Repairer licence issued by Consumer Protection WA. We verify credentials before onboarding.

💰

Upfront Pricing

You receive a firm price before any work begins. Perth Mechanic does not work with mechanics who quote low and charge high. Transparency is non-negotiable.
