Logbook Service Perth

Keep your new car warranty valid without paying dealership prices. Under Australian consumer law, any qualified mechanic can service your new car — you are not required to use the dealership. Our network mechanics stamp your manufacturer logbook using genuine or OEM-equivalent parts and fluids.

  • Manufacturer-specified oil and filter change
  • Fluid top-up (coolant, brake fluid, power steering)
  • Safety inspection across all major systems
  • Tyre rotation and pressure check
  • Logbook stamped with date and odometer reading
  • Includes service reminder sticker

Air Conditioning Service Perth

Perth summers are brutal. A functioning car air conditioner isn't a luxury — it's a safety necessity when temperatures exceed 40°C. Regular AC servicing prevents the far more expensive compressor repairs that result from running a low-refrigerant system.

  • AC re-gas (refrigerant recharge)
  • Leak detection and repair
  • Compressor diagnosis and replacement
  • Cabin air filter replacement
  • Condenser and evaporator inspection
  • Full system pressure test
