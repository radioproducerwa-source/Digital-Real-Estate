Using Insurance for Car Repairs in Perth: What You Need to Know

Dealing with a car insurance claim for repairs can be confusing — especially when your insurer steers you toward their preferred repairers. Perth drivers have more rights in this process than many realise.

Your Right to Choose Your Own Repairer

In Western Australia, comprehensive car insurance policies must allow you to choose your own licensed repairer. Insurers can recommend their preferred workshops, but they cannot force you to use them. If your insurer claims otherwise, review your Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) — your right to choose is typically written in.

Choosing an independent mechanic you trust can make a real difference in repair quality and communication. You deal directly with the person doing the work, not a call centre.

How the Claims Process Works

  1. Lodge your claim with your insurer promptly after the incident
  2. Your insurer will arrange an assessment — either at a repairer or via photos
  3. An assessor determines the repair scope and authorises costs
  4. Repairs proceed — at your chosen workshop if applicable
  5. Your insurer pays the repairer directly (minus your excess)

Fault vs Non-Fault Claims

If the accident wasn't your fault and the other driver is identified and insured, you may be able to recover your excess from their insurer — either directly or through your own insurer's recovery process. Keep all documentation from the incident.

Storm and Weather Damage in Perth

Perth experiences significant storm events that cause hail damage, fallen branch damage, and flood damage to vehicles. These are typically covered under comprehensive insurance. Document damage thoroughly with photos before moving the vehicle.

Repair Authorisation

Do not instruct your mechanic to start repairs before you have written authorisation from your insurer. Work done without authorisation may not be covered. Your mechanic and insurer communicate to agree on scope — a good workshop will handle this process smoothly.

What If You Disagree With the Assessment?

If you believe the insurer's assessment undervalues the repair scope, you can obtain an independent quote and dispute the assessment through the insurer's complaints process. If unresolved, escalate to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) — a free dispute resolution service.

Cash Settlements

In some cases, insurers offer a cash settlement instead of arranging repairs. This can be convenient but may undervalue the true repair cost. Get independent quotes before accepting a cash settlement if you're unsure.
