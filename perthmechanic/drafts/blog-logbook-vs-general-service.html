What Perth Drivers Should Know

Perth's climate, road conditions and driving patterns create specific demands on vehicles. Understanding these local factors helps you make better decisions about servicing and repairs.

The hot summers accelerate wear on cooling systems, tyres and air conditioning components. The long distances many Perth drivers cover — particularly in outer suburbs — mean higher annual mileage than the national average.
