Services & Event Types

Every event is different. Every client deserves a host who understands that. Here's how we work across every type of occasion — from intimate weddings to 500-person corporate galas.

Check Availability

💍 Wedding MC

Your wedding day is the most important event of your life — and your MC sets the tone for everything from the moment guests arrive. We work closely with you before the day to understand your story, your people, and your vision. On the night, we're the calm at the centre of the celebration.

  • Pre-wedding briefing & run sheet review
  • Ceremony MC (if required)
  • Bridal party introductions
  • Welcome speech & opening
  • Speech management & timing
  • First dance, cake cutting, bouquet cues
  • Vendor coordination (DJ, band, photographer)
  • Table service & meal transition management
  • Guest entertainment during transitions
  • Personalised script — no generic templates
  • Emergency contingency planning
  • On-the-night flexibility & adaptability

🏢 Corporate Events & Conferences

Corporate audiences have high expectations and low patience for amateur hosting. We understand brand tone, can facilitate panels and Q&As with authority, and keep long-day conferences energised without resorting to cringe-worthy icebreakers.

  • Conference & AGM hosting
  • Keynote & speaker introductions
  • Panel & Q&A facilitation
  • Awards presentation & scripting
  • Multi-session day management
  • Brand-aligned hosting style
  • Pre-event briefing with organiser
  • Speaker liaison & timing management
  • AV & technical coordination
  • Team energising & audience engagement
  • Christmas parties & year-end functions
  • Product launches & brand events

🏆 Gala Dinners, Awards Nights & Charity Events

Black tie events demand a different kind of presence — polished, commanding, and able to hold a room of dressed-up, discerning guests. Whether it's a charity gala, industry awards night, or fundraiser dinner, we bring the gravitas and warmth these events require.

  • Black tie event hosting
  • Awards ceremony scripting & presentation
  • Charity auction facilitation
  • VIP guest management
  • Dinner program management
  • Entertainment transitions
  • Fundraiser event hosting
  • Keynote & guest speaker support
  • Auction paddle & bidding facilitation
  • Cause storytelling & mission alignment
  • Table & seating management
  • Post-event announcements
Learn more about gala & charity MC services →

🎂 Milestone Birthdays & Private Parties

A 50th or 21st is a once-in-a-lifetime occasion. It deserves more than a playlist and a friend who "did a bit of public speaking once." We work with the family to weave the guest of honour's story through the evening — with warmth, humour, and perfectly timed emotion.

  • 50th & 21st birthday party hosting
  • Personalised tribute & roast elements
  • Video tribute & slideshow cuing
  • Family & friend speech management
  • Guest entertainment & games
  • Toast management & timing
  • Pre-event family briefing
  • Surprise element coordination
  • Photographer & DJ coordination
  • Cake cutting & milestone moments
  • Dance floor energy management
  • Any private function or celebration
Learn more about birthday MC services →

🎓 School Formals & Graduations

School formals are a big deal for students — and a logistical challenge for teachers. We handle all the formalities with energy and appropriate humour, keeping the night moving and the students engaged without overstepping the boundaries that make these events special.

  • Formal arrival & entry hosting
  • Year 12 King & Queen announcements
  • School leader & principal speeches
  • Dinner program management
  • Dance floor energy & engagement
  • Photo booth & activity cuing
  • Teacher & organiser briefing
  • Venue coordination
  • Age-appropriate tone & content
  • Crowd-appropriate engagement
  • End-of-night close & farewells
  • Year 10 formals & awards nights
