Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking a professional MC in Perth.

A professional MC (Master of Ceremonies) is the host of your event. They open the proceedings, introduce speakers or performers, manage transitions between parts of the program, keep energy levels up, handle any unexpected moments gracefully, and ensure the event runs to time. A great MC is invisible when things go right and invaluable when they don't.
MC pricing in Perth varies based on event type, duration, preparation required, and travel. As a general guide, wedding MC packages typically start from $800–$1,200, while corporate events and gala dinners range from $1,000–$2,500+. Contact us for a personalised quote — we're transparent about pricing and there are no hidden extras.
For weddings, we recommend booking at least 6–12 months in advance, especially for peak season dates (spring and summer). Corporate events and other functions can often be arranged with 4–8 weeks' notice. That said, if you have an urgent booking, always reach out — we may be able to accommodate last-minute enquiries.
Yes — and we treat each event type with equally thorough preparation. A wedding requires warmth, emotional intelligence, and precision timing. A corporate event requires brand awareness, professionalism, and the ability to engage a diverse audience. We tailor our style completely to your event and brief.
Our MC service includes: a detailed pre-event briefing call; run sheet review and advice; script preparation tailored to your event; coordination with your venue, AV team, and other vendors; on-the-night hosting from arrival to final farewells; and post-event debrief if required. Everything is customised — we don't use generic scripts.
Yes, we travel for events across Western Australia. Locations such as Mandurah, Rockingham, Fremantle, and the Swan Valley are no problem. For regional WA events (Bunbury, Margaret River, Albany, etc.) and interstate bookings, travel costs apply — contact us to discuss.
Absolutely. Run sheet advice is part of our service. If you have a draft, we'll review it and suggest improvements for timing and flow. If you're starting from scratch, we can help you build one based on your event format, guest numbers, and key moments. A tight run sheet is the foundation of a great event.
Experienced MCs earn their fee most during unexpected moments. Whether it's a speaker who overruns, AV issues, or a timing change, we stay calm, adapt, and keep your guests comfortable and entertained. We prepare contingency plans as part of every event briefing so that when things shift, we're already ahead of it.
