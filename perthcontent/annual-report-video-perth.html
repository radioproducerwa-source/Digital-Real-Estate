What Are Annual Report Videos?

Annual report videos are produced video summaries of an organisation's year — financial performance, strategic achievements, key milestones, community impact, and leadership outlook — presented in a format that is significantly more accessible and engaging than the printed or PDF annual report. Rather than asking investors, members, donors, or government stakeholders to read through dense financial documentation, an annual report video delivers the key narrative in 3–8 minutes of professionally produced content.

Annual report videos serve multiple functions simultaneously: they communicate with investors and shareholders in a format suited to digital distribution; they build community and narrative transparency for NFPs and charities; they demonstrate public accountability for government agencies and statutory bodies; and they create a permanent, shareable record of an organisation's year that can be referenced in future investor relations, grant applications, partnership conversations, and media enquiries.

Perth Content produces annual report videos that meet the communication objectives and governance standards of the organisations that require them — with all the precision, approvals workflow, and legal sensitivity that investor-grade and government reporting demands.

Who Needs Annual Report Video in Perth

ASX-listed and unlisted public companies with WA operations — particularly in resources, mining services, construction, property, and financial services — use annual report video as part of their investor relations communications package. A well-produced CEO message video embedded in the online annual report, distributed to the ASX announcement platform, and published on the company's investor relations website page significantly increases engagement with the annual results.

Large not-for-profit organisations — charities, foundations, housing associations, community service organisations, and peak bodies operating in Perth and across WA — increasingly use video annual reports to communicate their impact to donors, government funders, and community supporters. A 4–5 minute impact video that brings the numbers to life with real beneficiary stories is far more compelling as a funding conversation tool than a statistics-heavy printed document.

State and local government bodies, statutory authorities, and public sector agencies operating in Perth use annual report video for public accountability, ministerial communication, and stakeholder engagement. These productions require particular sensitivity to public sector communication standards and may involve additional approvals processes that Perth Content has experience navigating.

What to Include in an Annual Report Video

The most effective annual report videos combine four types of content. First, a CEO or Chair message to camera (1.5–3 minutes) that provides leadership context, acknowledges the year's challenges and achievements, and communicates strategic direction for the year ahead. This is the emotional anchor of the video — a leader speaking directly and authentically to their stakeholders.

Second, financial highlights visualised — key metrics presented through motion graphics (revenue, earnings, growth percentages, capital expenditure) in a way that makes the numbers memorable and communicates direction rather than just magnitude. Third, year-in-review highlights — operational milestones, project completions, strategic initiatives, significant partnerships, and awards or recognition, presented with appropriate B-roll footage of the work itself. Fourth, impact stories and human narrative — the people behind the numbers, whether that is employees, clients, beneficiaries, or community members whose lives were affected by the organisation's work during the year.

Typical Length and Format

Annual report videos typically run 3–8 minutes, with most sitting in the 4–6 minute range. This is long enough to deliver substantive content but short enough to hold the attention of investors, board members, donors, and other primary stakeholder audiences who are sophisticated, time-poor, and have high expectations of production quality.

A longer version (full AGM presentation, 15–30 minutes) can be produced for annual general meeting use and archived for member access. A shorter version (90-second social media cut) distributes key highlights to LinkedIn, Twitter, and stakeholder email lists. Perth Content delivers all required format versions from the same production, so organisations receive a complete communication package rather than a single deliverable.

Captioning and accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA standards) are standard inclusions in Perth Content's annual report video service, given the public accountability obligations of many of the organisations that commission this format.
