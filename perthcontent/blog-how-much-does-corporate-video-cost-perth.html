How Much Does Corporate Video Production Cost in Perth? (2025 Guide)

Corporate video pricing in Perth varies widely — from $500 testimonials to $10,000 brand films. Here's exactly what drives the cost and how to budget your next project.

📅 1 June 2026 🏷 Corporate ⏰ 6 min read

One of the most common questions Perth businesses ask us is: "How much should we budget for a corporate video?" The honest answer is that it depends — but that's not helpful on its own. In this guide we break down the real cost ranges for every type of corporate video in Perth, what drives price up or down, and how to get the most from your budget.

Corporate Video Pricing in Perth: Quick Reference

Here's a ballpark range for the most common corporate video types in the Perth market:

  • Testimonial video (1–2 min): $500 – $2,000
  • Social media content (30–60 sec): $300 – $1,500 per video
  • Product or service explainer (2–3 min): $1,500 – $4,000
  • Corporate overview / brand film (3–5 min): $3,000 – $10,000+
  • Training or internal video (5–15 min): $2,000 – $8,000
  • Event highlight video (3–5 min): $1,500 – $4,000
  • Drone footage add-on: $500 – $1,500

These ranges cover end-to-end production — scripting, filming, editing, colour grading, and delivery. Editing-only (where you supply the raw footage) is typically 30–50% less.

What Drives Corporate Video Costs Up

Understanding the cost levers helps you make smarter decisions about where to invest:

  • Crew size: A solo videographer costs far less than a full crew with a director, DOP, sound recordist, and production assistant.
  • Shoot days: More locations and scenes mean more shoot days — the biggest single cost driver.
  • Actors and talent: Professional on-screen talent adds $500–$2,000+ per day.
  • Animation and motion graphics: Custom animation is time-intensive — allow $150–$300 per hour of animation work.
  • Music licensing: Premium licensed tracks cost $200–$800. Royalty-free libraries are $30–$100.
  • Revisions: Most Perth video editors include 2 rounds of revisions. Additional rounds add cost.
  • Turnaround time: Rush projects (under 48 hours) typically attract a 25–50% surcharge.

What Keeps Costs Down Without Sacrificing Quality

With smart planning, Perth businesses regularly get broadcast-quality results at the lower end of these ranges. Here's how:

  • Supply your own footage: If your team can film with a decent camera or iPhone Pro, editing-only services cut costs significantly.
  • Single-location shoots: Choose one well-dressed location rather than five average ones.
  • Use existing team members: Authentic team testimonials often outperform scripted actor performances — and cost far less.
  • Batch your content: Shoot multiple videos in a single day to spread the crew and setup cost across more deliverables.
  • Repurpose one long video: A 3-minute brand film can be cut into 5–8 shorter social media clips at minimal extra cost.

Editing-Only vs Full Production: Which Do You Need?

Many Perth businesses already have footage — from their marketing team, phone shoots, or previous productions. In these cases, an editing-only service is the most cost-effective option:

  • Editing only (1–3 min video): $300 – $1,200
  • Editing only (5–10 min video): $800 – $2,500
  • Colour grading + sound mix: Add $200 – $600
  • Subtitles/captions: Add $100 – $300

Perth Content specialises in editing-only engagements with fast 48-hour turnaround — making it ideal for businesses with an in-house content pipeline that needs professional polish.

How to Get Accurate Quotes from Perth Video Editors

To get useful quotes (rather than vague ranges), come prepared with:

  • Final video length or approximate length
  • Number of filming locations
  • Whether you need filming or editing-only
  • Intended use (website, social media, internal, TV)
  • Deadline and turnaround requirements
  • Any references or examples of videos you like

The more specific your brief, the more accurate your quote — and the less likely you are to face scope creep and unexpected charges later.

Is Cheaper Always Worse?

Not necessarily. A focused $800 testimonial video shot on an iPhone with professional editing and good lighting can outperform a $5,000 over-produced brand film that feels stiff and corporate. What matters most is authenticity, clear messaging, and strong editing — not the size of the budget.

That said, if your video represents your brand at conferences, on your homepage, or in sales meetings, investing in professional production pays dividends. First impressions matter.
