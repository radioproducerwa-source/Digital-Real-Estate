What Are Instagram Reels?

Instagram Reels are short-form vertical videos up to 90 seconds long, displayed in a dedicated Reels tab and pushed into the main Instagram feed and Explore page. Unlike regular feed posts, Reels are actively distributed to non-followers — making them the only format on Instagram with genuine organic reach potential for accounts that want to grow.

Meta has consistently prioritised Reels in its algorithm since the format launched, and data across industries shows that businesses posting Reels regularly outperform those relying only on static images or standard videos in follower growth, profile visits, and website link clicks.

For Perth businesses — especially in hospitality, retail, fitness, real estate, and trades — Reels represent a low-cost, high-return marketing channel that, when done consistently and professionally, can replace or supplement significant paid advertising spend.

Perth has a highly engaged Instagram user base. The city's outdoor lifestyle, food scene, and business community all translate naturally into visually compelling short-form content. A Mount Lawley cafe's morning preparation Reel, a Subiaco boutique's new arrivals showcase, or a Joondalup tradesperson's satisfying job completion clip — all of these can reach thousands of Perth users who would never otherwise see the business's content.

That kind of distribution from a single piece of organic content is unmatched by any other format currently available.

Perth Content helps businesses capitalise on this by producing Reels that are genuinely optimised for performance — not just "good enough" video, but content that is structured, paced, and presented in the way the algorithm and Perth audiences reward.

Optimal Reel Length, Format, and Hooks

The sweet spot for Instagram Reels in most industries is 15–30 seconds. This length is long enough to deliver a meaningful message but short enough to hold attention through to the end — a key metric Instagram uses to determine reach. Reels above 60 seconds tend to see sharply lower completion rates unless the content is exceptionally compelling throughout.

Format must be vertical (9:16 aspect ratio) with safe zones kept clear of the bottom captions and side edge. Your hook — the first 1–3 seconds — is the most important element. It determines whether a viewer continues watching or swipes to the next Reel. Strong hooks use visual disruption, curiosity gaps, relatable scenarios, or bold on-screen text that promises value.

Perth Content editors structure every Reel with a deliberate hook-message-CTA framework. We also advise on content ideas that perform well for your specific Perth niche so you are never starting from a blank page.

Music, Captions, and Cover Images

Audio is the backbone of Reels performance. Instagram's algorithm gives preference to Reels that use trending audio — specific songs or sounds that are currently being widely used across the platform. Perth Content monitors trending audio weekly and selects appropriate tracks for your content, or can sync your footage to audio you specify.

Captions (on-screen text) are essential for two reasons: a large proportion of Reels are watched without sound, and captions dramatically increase watch time by reinforcing the spoken message visually. We include animated or pop-style captions as a standard part of every Reel edit, matching your brand fonts and colours where guidelines are provided.

Cover images — the thumbnail shown on your grid — are often neglected but matter for profile consistency. We include a clean cover frame as part of every edit so your Reels look intentional and brand-consistent on your profile grid.

Posting Frequency and Content Strategy

Most Perth businesses see meaningful growth with three to five Reels per week. This requires a production system — batch filming sessions followed by efficient editing — rather than trying to produce individual Reels one at a time. Perth Content helps clients design batching workflows that yield a week or two of Reels content from a single 1–2 hour filming session.

A typical content mix might include: one educational or tip-based Reel, one behind-the-scenes or process Reel, one promotional or product Reel, and one personality or culture Reel per week. This variety prevents audience fatigue while hitting the content types that perform best across different viewer segments. We can work from your existing filming or brief your team on what to capture.
