Why Perth Businesses Choose Professional Reels Editing

Great Reels aren't just filmed well — they're edited to perform.

  • ▶Organic reach to non-followers — Reels are shown to users beyond your existing followers, making them the most powerful organic discovery tool on Instagram.
  • ▶Mobile-first vertical format (9:16) — Properly formatted for full-screen mobile viewing with safe zones respected so nothing important gets cropped.
  • ▶Hook-first editing structure — The first 2-3 seconds determine whether a viewer stays or scrolls. Our editors build every Reel around a compelling opening hook.
  • ▶Trending audio integration — The right audio track can dramatically increase a Reel's reach. We stay current on what's performing on the platform.
  • ▶Branded captions and overlays — On-screen text that matches your brand fonts and colours, ensuring every Reel looks unmistakably like you.
  • ▶First 3-second hook optimisation — Editing decisions specifically designed to stop the scroll and earn those precious extra seconds of watch time.
