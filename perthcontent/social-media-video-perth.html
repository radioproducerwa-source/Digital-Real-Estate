Social Video Is the Default Marketing Channel for Perth Businesses

Perth businesses in hospitality, retail, real estate, fitness, professional services, and virtually every other sector now use social media video as a primary marketing channel. The question is no longer whether to post video — it is how to produce it consistently at a quality level that builds rather than undermines your brand.

Each platform has its own content norms, optimal lengths, aspect ratios, and algorithmic preferences. A video that performs brilliantly as a TikTok will not necessarily translate directly to LinkedIn without adjustment. Perth Content understands these platform differences and edits your content specifically for where it will be seen.

Our social media video editing service takes your raw footage — whether shot on a phone, DSLR, or professional camera — and transforms it into finished, platform-ready content. Colour grade, sound mix, captions, music, and platform-specific cuts are all included.

Platform Overview: What Works Where

Instagram is Perth's most popular visual social platform for consumer-facing businesses. Reels (vertical, 15–90 seconds) are prioritised in the feed and Explore page, making them the highest organic reach format available. Stories (vertical, up to 60 seconds) are ideal for behind-the-scenes and time-sensitive offers. Feed videos (up to 60 seconds) suit slightly more polished brand content. For dedicated Reels editing, see our Instagram Reels editing Perth service.

TikTok continues to grow rapidly among Perth audiences aged 18–45. Short-form vertical video (15–60 seconds) with strong audio hooks dominates, and the platform rewards frequency and authenticity over high production polish. Perth businesses in food, fitness, retail, and trades are finding real traction on TikTok. See our TikTok video editing Perth page for more.

LinkedIn is the platform of choice for B2B Perth businesses, professional services firms, and corporate brands. Native video — posted directly to LinkedIn rather than linking to YouTube — dramatically outperforms other post types for reach. Optimal length is 30–90 seconds. YouTube is the second-largest search engine and suits longer-form content (tutorials, explainers, vlogs, reviews). It requires a different editing approach, with stronger attention to retention across 5–15 minute videos.

What Makes Good Social Video

The single most important element in any social video is the hook — the first 1–3 seconds that determine whether the viewer keeps watching or scrolls past. Perth Content editors are trained to prioritise the hook above all else, whether that means starting mid-action, opening with a bold visual, or leading with a provocative caption overlay.

Beyond the hook, good social video has a clear single message (not three messages), pacing that matches the platform's energy, music that supports rather than distracts, and a clear call to action at the end. Captions are now essential across all platforms — a large proportion of social video is watched without sound, and captioned videos consistently outperform uncaptioned ones in engagement metrics.

We also advise on content types that perform well for your specific industry and audience in Perth. A tradie's before-and-after video performs very differently from a restaurant's chef-in-action Reel, even though both are short-form social content — and our editors understand those differences intimately.

Content Pillars and Posting Frequency

Sustainable social media growth requires a consistent posting rhythm built around two to four content pillars — recurring themes that define what your brand talks about. For a Perth fitness studio those pillars might be: workout demos, member transformations, nutrition tips, and behind-the-scenes content. For a Perth law firm: explainers on common legal issues, client success stories, team culture, and industry commentary.

Most Perth businesses should aim for three to five short-form social videos per week across their primary platforms. This sounds like a lot, but when one filming session yields raw material that can be cut into multiple platform-specific versions, the production cost per post drops significantly.
