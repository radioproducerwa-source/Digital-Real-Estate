About Perth Content

Perth's trusted video editing and production team — serving Perth businesses since 2023.

Our Story

Perth Content was born out of a simple observation: Perth's businesses, real estate agents, and couples were sitting on great raw footage with no easy way to turn it into polished, professional video content. The agencies were too expensive. The freelancers were hard to find and unpredictable. The gap was obvious.

Founded in 2023, we set out to build Perth's go-to video editing business — broadcast-quality video editing at transparent prices, with fast turnarounds and zero hassle. Our team is made up of Perth's most talented video editors, each vetted for quality, reliability, and local market knowledge.

What started as a small operation serving real estate agents in Subiaco quickly grew into something much bigger. Today we serve 500+ Perth clients across every industry — from café owners in Mount Lawley to property developers in Cottesloe to couples celebrating their weddings on the Swan River.

We understand Perth. We know the neighbourhoods, the market, and what local audiences respond to. That local knowledge, combined with professional editing standards, is what makes our content actually work.

Our Mission

Our mission is to democratise professional video content for Perth businesses of every size.

For too long, high-quality video production was the exclusive domain of large businesses with large budgets. A sole trader in Victoria Park shouldn't have to settle for shaky phone footage just because they can't afford an agency retainer. A real estate agent in Joondalup shouldn't lose listings to competitors who invest in professional video while they don't.

Perth Content exists to level the playing field. We believe every Perth business deserves access to professional video content at a price that makes sense — and we've built our entire model around making that a reality. Transparent pricing, no lock-in contracts, fast turnarounds, and a satisfaction guarantee that means you never pay for work that doesn't meet your expectations.
