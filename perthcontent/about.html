Our Mission

Perth Content was built to make professional video production accessible to every Perth business. Too often, businesses either pay agency-level prices for video they don't need, or they settle for amateur-looking content that doesn't reflect their brand. We built a better way.

Our network of experienced local video professionals covers every type of content — from corporate brand films to Instagram Reels, real estate walkthroughs to event highlights, explainer animations to YouTube series. Whatever your business needs, we have the right professional for the job.

We're not a one-size-fits-all agency. We're a connector — and that means better outcomes for Perth businesses of every size and industry.

How We Work

We're a connector, not a single studio. When you submit a brief, we match you with the right video professional for your project based on your industry, budget, timeline and creative requirements.

The result: better outcomes, faster turnaround, and pricing that fits your budget. No agency overhead, no being passed from account manager to account manager. Just direct access to experienced Perth video professionals who are right for your brief.

Every professional in our network has been reviewed for quality, reliability and communication. We only work with people we'd recommend to our own clients.

Why Perth Businesses Are Investing in Video in 2025

The shift to video is accelerating. Here's why Perth businesses are making it a priority.

  • ✓
    Video dominates social media reach

    Platforms like Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook now heavily favour video content in their algorithms. Businesses posting video consistently are seeing significantly better organic reach than those posting static images alone.

  • ✓
    Perth's property market relies on it

    Real estate agents across Perth who use professional property video are reporting faster sales, higher enquiry rates and better price outcomes — especially in the premium and off-plan market segments.

  • ✓
    Buyers watch before they buy

    Research consistently shows that consumers are more likely to purchase after watching a product or service video. For Perth B2B and B2C businesses alike, video is now a key part of the conversion journey.

  • ✓
    Remote work has changed internal comms

    With hybrid and remote work now standard across Perth's corporate sector, training videos, company updates and leadership messages delivered via video have become essential communication tools.

  • ✓
    Production costs have dropped significantly

    Advances in camera technology, editing software and streamlined production processes mean professional-quality video is now accessible to businesses of every size — not just those with large marketing budgets.
