Product videos are one of the highest-converting pieces of content an e-commerce or retail business can have. Customers who watch a product video are significantly more likely to purchase than those who only see static images. For Perth businesses selling physical products — whether online or in-store — a professional product video builds trust, answers questions, and makes the buying decision easier.

The reason product video works so well is simple: it closes the gap between what people want to know about a product and what a photo can show them. How big is it really? How does it feel in use? How do I assemble it? What does it look like from different angles? A well-made product video answers all of these questions in under 90 seconds — reducing hesitation and reducing returns.

Perth Content connects product sellers with videographers and editors who understand what makes product content convert. They know the lighting setups that make surfaces and textures look their best, the camera movements that communicate quality and scale, and the editing pace that keeps viewers engaged without losing the key details they need to see before buying.

Whether you're selling on your own website, Amazon Australia, through social media ads or in-store displays, we can help you create product video content that works across every channel. A single product shoot day can yield multiple formats — product page demos, lifestyle videos, social clips and ad variants — making it one of the most efficient content investments a Perth product business can make.

Why Perth Businesses Choose Product Video

Product video delivers measurable improvement to conversion rates and customer satisfaction.

  • ▶Higher conversion rates on product pages — Visitors who watch a product video are more likely to add to cart and less likely to abandon the page without converting.
  • ▶Reduces return rates through clear expectations — When customers understand exactly what they're buying — size, texture, function — they're less likely to be surprised and disappointed when it arrives.
  • ▶Works on website, Amazon, social ads and email — One set of product videos serves your entire sales and marketing ecosystem — no need to create separate content for each channel.
  • ▶Builds confidence in online shoppers — Video conveys quality cues — materials, construction, finish — that photography often can't. Shoppers who can see a product clearly are more confident buyers.
  • ▶Shows features and benefits static images can't — Movement, interaction, assembly, size in context — all things that require video to communicate clearly.
  • ▶Repurposable for multiple channels — Product shoot content can be cut for ads, social media, email campaigns, influencer briefing packs and wholesale buyer presentations.
