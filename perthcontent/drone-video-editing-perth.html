Perth's coastline, property developments, and sprawling landscapes make drone footage a powerful storytelling tool for businesses across the city. Whether you're a real estate agent marketing a premium property, a developer showcasing a construction site, or a venue operator wanting to show off your location, professional drone footage — properly edited — creates an immediate visual impact that ground-level footage simply can't match.

Raw drone footage, however, is rarely ready to use as-is. The clips need to be selected, sequenced, colour graded, and combined with music and ground-level footage to tell a cohesive story. Unedited aerial footage can actually work against you — it takes too long, lacks narrative flow, and the flat, Log-profile footage looks washed out without professional colour grading.

Perth Content connects you with editors who specialise in aerial footage — understanding the unique qualities of drone cinematography, the optimal way to transition between aerial and ground-level shots, and how to grade drone footage to achieve that cinematic, golden-hour quality that makes property and venue marketing genuinely compelling.

Our network also includes CASA-certified drone operators for clients who need both filming and editing. Whether you have existing drone footage that needs professional editing, or you need a drone operator and editor working together on your project, we can help match you with the right professional for your location, timing and budget.

Why Perth Businesses Choose Drone Video

Aerial footage delivers a perspective that immediately elevates any visual marketing.

  • ▶Unique perspective unavailable from ground level — Aerial footage shows scale, surrounds, context and relationship to landmarks in a way no ground-based camera can replicate.
  • ▶Instantly elevates production quality — Even a few well-edited aerial shots transform the perceived production quality of an entire video — giving your brand a premium, established impression.
  • ▶Shows property surrounds and context — For real estate and development, aerial footage communicates proximity to beaches, parks, schools and amenities better than any floor plan or map.
  • ▶Creates premium marketing impression — Properties and venues with drone footage consistently attract more enquiries and stronger first impressions from prospective buyers and tenants.
  • ▶Ideal for real estate and development projects — Perth's property market is competitive. Aerial footage is a standard expectation for premium listings and an advantage for mid-market properties.
  • ▶Shareable, attention-grabbing social content — Drone footage consistently outperforms ground-level video for engagement on social media — particularly on Instagram and LinkedIn.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our network includes drone operators who can both fly and edit, or editing-only services for footage you've already captured. If you need a drone operator, we can match you with a CASA-licensed professional with appropriate insurance for commercial work in Perth.

Commercial drone operations require the operator to hold a Remote Pilot Licence (RePL) and may require airspace authorisation from CASA, particularly near controlled airspace. All drone professionals in our network hold the appropriate CASA licences and certifications for commercial operations.

Perth Airport creates a controlled airspace zone requiring CASA authorisation. Some residential areas and public parks have operational restrictions. Coastal zones near popular beaches often have restrictions during peak periods. Our drone operators are familiar with Perth's airspace and handle all necessary approvals as part of the service.

Editing-only for existing drone footage typically starts from $150-$300 for a short property video. Full drone production including the flight session and complete editing usually starts around $400-$800 for a standard residential property session. Larger commercial or development projects are quoted individually based on scope and complexity.
