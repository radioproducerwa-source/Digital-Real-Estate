An explainer video takes your most complex service, product or process and makes it instantly understandable. For Perth businesses with offerings that need explanation — software platforms, financial services, consulting, healthcare, professional services — an explainer video is often the highest-converting piece of content on your website.

The challenge most Perth businesses face isn't a lack of great products or services. It's that their potential customers don't immediately grasp the value. A well-crafted explainer video closes that gap in 60 to 90 seconds. It answers the question "what is this?" before a visitor's attention drifts, and it makes the decision to enquire feel logical rather than uncertain.

Perth Content connects you with video professionals who specialise in explainer content — from live action shoots that put a human face on your service, to animated explainers that visualise abstract concepts and technical processes in a way that's immediately clear. Our network includes specialists in software product demos, whiteboard-style animation, and motion graphic explainers across a range of budgets.

Explainer videos that sit prominently on your website homepage or key landing pages typically reduce bounce rates, increase time-on-page and — most importantly — drive more enquiries. They also work equally well in email campaigns, sales proposals, LinkedIn posts and paid advertising. A good explainer video is one of the most versatile assets in your marketing toolkit.

Why Perth Businesses Choose Explainer Videos

A clear, compelling explainer video pays dividends across your entire marketing funnel.

  • ▶Converts website visitors into leads — Visitors who watch an explainer video are significantly more likely to complete a contact form or make a purchase than those who don't.
  • ▶Reduces customer service enquiries — When people understand exactly what you offer and how it works before they contact you, enquiries are more qualified and support volume drops.
  • ▶Works 24/7 on your website and social — Your explainer video sells for you while you sleep — answering questions and building confidence at any hour.
  • ▶Scales your sales pitch consistently — Every prospect gets the same clear, compelling message — no variation in how your offering is described or understood.
  • ▶Repurposable across ads, email and social — One well-made explainer video can be cut for ads, shared on social, embedded in email sequences and used in sales proposals.
  • ▶Works for B2B and B2C audiences — Whether you're explaining a B2B software platform or a consumer product, the fundamentals of a clear, structured explainer apply equally.
