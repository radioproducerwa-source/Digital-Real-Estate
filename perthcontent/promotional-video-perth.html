What Is a Promotional Video?

A promotional video is any video created primarily to drive a specific audience action — a purchase, a booking, an enquiry, a sign-up, or an event attendance. Unlike brand films (which build long-term awareness and association) or explainer videos (which educate), promotional videos are campaign-oriented: they have a clear offer, a defined audience, a deadline or urgency element, and a direct call to action.

Promo videos range from 15-second paid social ads to 90-second launch films. The format, length, and production approach depend entirely on the campaign objective, the target audience, and the distribution channel. Perth Content helps clients match the right format to the right objective so the production investment is efficiently applied.

Perth businesses across retail, professional services, events, hospitality, fitness, and eCommerce use promotional video as a core campaign asset — delivered through paid social, email marketing, website banners, and organic social channels. Perth Content produces promotional content for all of these applications.

Use Cases: Launch, Sale, and Brand Awareness

Product and service launch videos are among the most high-stakes promotional formats. A great launch video creates anticipation, communicates the core value proposition in under 60 seconds, and motivates viewers to take immediate action — whether that is pre-ordering, signing up for a waitlist, or making a first purchase. Launch videos are typically multi-format: a 60-second hero version, a 15-second teaser for paid ads, and a 30-second social cut for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Sale and promotional event videos create urgency. The visual language of a great sale video — bold typography, energetic pacing, countdown energy — signals to the viewer that there is a time-limited opportunity. Perth businesses running EOFY sales, Black Friday campaigns, seasonal promotions, or opening-week events use promotional video to drive traffic and spike enquiry volume during the campaign window.

Brand awareness promotional videos are less transactional in tone but still campaign-oriented. They aim to build recognition, establish positioning, and create associations in the Perth market — "we are the brand that does X for people like you." This format is particularly effective for new-to-market businesses, rebrands, and businesses entering a new segment.

What Separates Great Promo Video From Average

The most common failure in promotional video is trying to say too much. A 60-second promo that covers six product features, three pricing options, two target audiences, and a generic CTA will convert nobody. Great promotional video identifies the single most compelling reason this specific audience should act right now and builds the entire 60 seconds around communicating that one thing with maximum clarity and emotional resonance.

Music is the emotional multiplier in promotional video. The right track can make a sale feel exciting, a new product feel aspirational, or a local business feel warm and approachable. Perth Content licences professional music and works with clients to select tracks that match not just the tone of the video but the brand personality and the target audience's sensibility.

The call to action must be specific and frictionless. "Visit our website" is weak. "Order online today — free Perth delivery" is strong. Perth Content works with clients at the scripting stage to ensure the CTA matches the campaign goal and the landing page it points to.

Script, Music, and Call to Action

Perth Content's promotional video service includes scripting support as part of full-production packages. A strong promotional script follows a simple framework: hook (why should the viewer care?), value proposition (what is the offer?), proof or credibility (why should they trust this?), and CTA (what should they do now?). This framework works whether the video is 15 seconds or 90 seconds — it simply scales to fit the format.

For clients providing their own script or concept, we provide creative feedback at the brief stage before production begins. Music selection and synchronisation are handled as part of every edit, with options presented for client approval before the final assembly cut.

Distribution Strategy for Perth Promotional Video

A promotional video without a distribution plan is a tree falling in an empty forest. Perth Content helps clients think about where their video will be seen — paid social (Facebook and Instagram ads, YouTube pre-roll), organic social posts, email marketing, website landing page embedding, or out-of-home digital screens — and ensures the edit is formatted and optimised for each channel. A single promotional video shoot can yield a 16:9 website version, a 1:1 square for Instagram feed, a 9:16 vertical for Stories and Reels, and a horizontal cut for YouTube pre-roll ads, all from the same source footage.
