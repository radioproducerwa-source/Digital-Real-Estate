A great promotional video compresses your best marketing message into 30 to 90 seconds and puts it in front of the right audience. For Perth businesses running campaigns, launching products, promoting events or growing brand awareness, a professional promo video is the most versatile and effective marketing asset available.

The difference between a promotional video that performs and one that doesn't usually comes down to clarity of message and quality of execution. The best promo videos know exactly who they're talking to, what they want that person to feel or do, and how to deliver that message quickly and memorably. Production quality matters — but a sharp, clear message delivered with good energy and competent production will always outperform a beautiful video with nothing to say.

Perth Content connects you with video professionals who combine creative and commercial skills. They understand how to tell a brand story in a format that creates action — not just admiration. Whether you need a punchy 15-second social ad or a two-minute brand awareness film, they approach the work with the same rigour: understand the objective, serve the audience, deliver the result.

Promotional videos are also among the most adaptable content investments a Perth business can make. A single promotional shoot can be cut into multiple lengths and formats — a 90-second brand video, a 30-second social cut, a 15-second ad variant, and a set of still frames for supporting social graphics. The production cost is amortised across an entire campaign's worth of content.

Why Perth Businesses Choose Promotional Video

A strong promo video is your best salesperson — working across every channel, around the clock.

  • ▶High-impact marketing in a short format — A 30-90 second promotional video can communicate more brand value and create more desire than any other single piece of marketing content.
  • ▶Drives direct enquiry and sales — Well-targeted promo videos with clear calls to action directly drive phone calls, website visits and purchase decisions.
  • ▶Works across TV, social, website and email — A promotional video produced to the right brief works across every channel in your marketing mix without needing significant adaptation.
  • ▶Creates urgency around promotions and launches — Video is the most effective format for creating a sense of energy and momentum around a campaign, launch or limited-time offer.
  • ▶Professionally positions your brand — A polished promotional video signals that you're an established, credible business — building trust with prospects who don't yet know you.
  • ▶Easily updated for seasonal campaigns — A strong base production can be re-edited with updated offers, pricing and seasonal messaging — extending the life of your investment.
