Types of Fitness and Wellness Video Content

Workout demonstration videos are the bread and butter of fitness content. A well-edited exercise demo — showing proper form, technique cues, and modification options — positions a personal trainer or gym as an authority while providing genuine value to the audience. Workout demos work across all platforms: YouTube for detailed instructional content, Instagram Reels for 30-second "try this move" posts, and TikTok for trending workout formats like "7 exercises for X" or "train with me" day-in-the-life content.

Transformation stories are among the most powerful content types in fitness marketing — when handled with appropriate care and consent. A genuine client transformation story, with the client sharing their experience in their own words, supported by before-and-after visuals, is compelling social proof that can be more persuasive than any amount of coach credentials or gym marketing claims. Perth Content advises on the ethical framing of transformation content and the consent language required before filming.

Class and session promo videos are essential for group fitness businesses — yoga studios, Pilates studios, boxing gyms, CrossFit boxes, dance studios, and bootcamp operators. A 30–60 second class promo that captures the energy, the community, and the experience of attending draws in prospective members who are on the fence about signing up. Perth's fitness market is highly competitive, and a well-produced class promo is a meaningful differentiator.

What Perth Gyms and PTs Need From Video

Perth's fitness landscape spans everything from large franchise gyms (Goodlife, Anytime Fitness, F45) to boutique independent studios, outdoor bootcamp operators, and sole-trader personal trainers working from home gym setups or at facilities across the city. Each of these business types has different video needs and different platform priorities.

Sole-trader PTs and small studios benefit most from consistent, personal social media video — content that builds a relationship between the trainer and their prospective client base over time. Platforms: Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Facebook Groups. Larger gyms and franchise operators benefit from more produced brand content — class promos, facility showcases, member stories — that can be used in paid advertising and website presentation. Perth Content works with both scales of fitness business and prices accordingly.

Coach profile videos — 60–120 second introductions that let a trainer's personality come through on camera — are one of the most impactful and underused formats in Perth's fitness market. A genuinely engaging coach profile video on an Instagram profile or website converts visitors who are shopping for a PT more effectively than any credential list or transformation photo gallery.

Before and After Content: Ethics and Best Practices

Before-and-after transformation content is effective but requires careful handling. Australian guidelines from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and the Australian Association of National Advertisers (AANA) apply to fitness and wellness marketing claims, particularly when results are implied to be typical or when supplements, products, or medical interventions are involved. Perth Content advises all fitness clients to include appropriate disclosures with transformation content and to frame results as individual outcomes rather than typical expectations.

Written informed consent from subjects appearing in transformation content is essential, and Perth Content provides a simple consent framework as part of our standard pre-production process. Properly handled, transformation content is among the most powerful fitness marketing available. Handled poorly, it creates compliance and reputational risk.

Outdoor Perth Locations for Fitness Content

Perth's climate and outdoor environments are genuine advantages for fitness video production. The beach — Cottesloe, City Beach, Scarborough, Mettams Pool — provides world-class fitness content backdrops for outdoor training sessions, sunrise runs, and ocean workout content. Kings Park, Centennial Park, John Forrest National Park, and dozens of suburban parks and reserves offer diverse natural settings for outdoor training content.

Perth's year-round sunshine means outdoor fitness filming is possible in almost any month without weather contingency planning. Golden hour (one hour after sunrise or before sunset) in Perth is particularly spectacular, producing warm, cinematic light that elevates even smartphone-captured fitness content to a professional standard. Perth Content advisors can help plan filming sessions to maximise the visual quality of outdoor Perth locations.
