Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our video editing services in Perth.

Most projects are turned around within 24–48 hours of receiving your footage. More complex projects — such as full cinematic wedding films or multi-day corporate productions — typically take 3–5 business days. Rush delivery is available on request for an additional fee.
We accept all major formats including MP4, MOV, MXF, AVCHD, and RAW footage from drones (DJI, Autel) and professional cameras (Sony, Canon, Blackmagic). Deliveries are provided as H.264 or H.265 MP4 files optimised for your intended platform, unless you request an alternative such as ProRes.
After booking, we'll send you a secure upload link. You can upload directly from your computer or phone, or share a Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer link. For large projects we can also arrange alternative transfer methods including hard drive delivery.
Every project includes two rounds of revisions at no extra cost. A revision covers changes such as trimming, music swaps, colour adjustments, caption edits, or reordering clips. Structural re-edits or significant scope changes may attract a small additional fee, which we'll always discuss upfront.
Yes. All music used in your videos is sourced from licensed libraries (Artlist, Epidemic Sound, or similar) and is cleared for commercial use across social media, websites, and broadcast. You won't face copyright strikes on YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok.
While our core service is professional video editing, we can connect you with trusted Perth-based videographers and drone operators through our network. Get in touch and we'll help you put together the right team for your project from start to finish.
