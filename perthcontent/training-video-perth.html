Types of Training Videos We Produce

Onboarding videos introduce new employees to your company culture, systems, policies, and expectations before their first day or in their first week. A structured 5–10 video onboarding series replaces hours of face-to-face induction, frees up managers' time, and ensures every new hire receives the same consistent information regardless of when they start or who is available to train them.

Compliance training videos cover mandatory topics such as workplace health and safety, anti-discrimination and harassment policy, data privacy, and industry-specific regulatory requirements. Video format ensures completeness and allows for completion tracking when hosted on an LMS — critical for organisations that need audit-ready evidence of training delivery. Perth businesses in mining, construction, healthcare, and financial services particularly rely on compliance video.

Skills and process training videos teach specific tasks: how to use a piece of software, how to operate equipment, how to handle a customer complaint, how to complete a specific procedure. These videos are most valuable when a process is performed frequently, is prone to variation across employees, or carries risk if performed incorrectly. How-to videos for customer-facing use (product tutorials, setup guides, FAQ responses) reduce support burden and improve customer satisfaction.

Benefits of Training Video Over In-Person Training

In-person training has a fundamental scalability problem: it requires a qualified trainer, a scheduled time, and a physical or virtual space — every time a new person needs to be trained. Video training is produced once and deployed indefinitely, which means the cost per training completion drops with every use. For Perth businesses that hire seasonally, experience high turnover in specific roles, or operate across multiple locations, this scalability advantage is transformative.

Video training also improves learning retention. Learners can pause, rewind, and re-watch sections they do not immediately understand — something that is disruptive in a classroom setting but natural in a video context. When combined with quizzes, interactive elements, and completion tracking on an LMS, video training can achieve measurably better knowledge retention than lecture-style delivery.

Perth Content works with HR managers, L&D professionals, and operations teams at Perth businesses of all sizes to develop training video content that is genuinely effective — engaging to watch, clear to understand, and efficient to produce.

LMS Compatibility and Delivery Formats

Most Perth organisations hosting training videos use a Learning Management System (LMS) such as TalentLMS, Docebo, Canvas, or a SharePoint-based internal platform. Perth Content delivers training videos in the formats required by your specific system — typically MP4 at 1080p with h.264 encoding for broad compatibility, with alternative codecs available on request.

For organisations needing SCORM-compatible training modules (self-contained interactive packages that track completion and quiz scores within an LMS), we can coordinate with e-learning developers to wrap your Perth Content video into a full SCORM module. We also advise on chapter structuring and video length to optimise engagement — most effective training videos are 4–12 minutes long, broken into topic-specific chapters rather than delivered as a single long-form video.

Screen Recording + Presenter Combinations

Many of the most effective training videos for software and digital process training combine a screen recording showing the system in action with a picture-in-picture presenter who guides the viewer through each step. This combination maintains the personal connection of a human trainer while clearly showing the exact interface the learner needs to navigate.

Perth Content handles both screen capture (using Camtasia, Loom, or OBS-recorded footage) and camera footage from your in-house presenter, synchronising them into a polished final edit with callout graphics, zoom animations on screen elements, and branded lower thirds. We can also work from voiceover scripts if your presenter prefers to record audio only and have graphics and screen recordings assembled around the narration.

Training Video Pricing in Perth

Training video production pricing varies significantly based on content complexity and delivery format. A single module edit from existing footage or screen recordings starts from $400 for a 5–10 minute video with basic titles, graphics, and colour correction. Full-service training video production including scriptwriting, presenter recording coordination, screen capture, motion graphics overlays, and LMS-ready delivery ranges from $800–$1,200 per module. Multi-module series attract volume pricing.
