Why Your Perth Business Needs Video Content in 2025

Video is no longer optional for Perth businesses. Here's why video content drives more leads, builds more trust, and converts better than any other medium — and how to get started without a big budget.

📅 8 June 2026 🏷 Strategy ⏰ 5 min read

A few years ago, video was a nice-to-have for Perth businesses with a bigger marketing budget. Today, it's table stakes. Consumers expect video before they buy. Google rewards pages with video. And on every social platform, video content gets dramatically more reach than static posts. If your Perth business isn't producing video content in 2025, you're leaving leads — and revenue — on the table.

The Numbers Don't Lie

Before we get into the why, consider the scale of the shift:

  • Video content generates 49% more revenue growth for businesses that use it strategically.
  • Landing pages with video convert at up to 80% higher rates than those without.
  • 87% of marketers say video has directly increased their website traffic.
  • Social media posts with video get 48% more views than text or image posts.
  • After watching a product video, 74% of users are more likely to buy.

These aren't vanity metrics. They translate directly to enquiries, phone calls, and sales.

Why Perth Businesses Specifically Benefit from Video

Perth is a relationship-driven market. Business here runs on trust, referrals, and reputation — and nothing builds those faster than seeing the real people behind a business. A two-minute brand video showing your team, your workspace, and real client results does more trust-building work than six months of text-only social posts.

Perth is also a geographically isolated market. Many businesses serve clients across a wide area — from the CBD to Joondalup, Fremantle to the Swan Valley. Video lets you demonstrate expertise and personality at scale, reaching prospects you'll never meet face-to-face before they decide to enquire.

The 5 Biggest Ways Video Grows Your Perth Business

1. Video Keeps People on Your Website Longer

The average visitor spends 88% more time on a website that has video. More time on site means more trust built, and Google treats longer session durations as a positive ranking signal — boosting your SEO at the same time.

2. Video Explains What Text Can't

Complex services — construction processes, financial products, professional services, technical equipment — are hard to explain in text. An explainer video can make a complicated concept instantly clear in 90 seconds. That clarity reduces buyer hesitation and shortens your sales cycle.

3. Video Builds Trust Before the First Contact

Buyers research before they reach out. If your competitors have polished video testimonials and you don't, they've already built more trust before a single conversation happens. A testimonial video featuring a real Perth client is worth ten written reviews.

4. Video Dominates Social Media Reach

LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok all actively push video content over static posts in their algorithms. A 60-second Reel or LinkedIn video can reach 10x more people than a text post — at zero extra spend.

5. Video Multiplies Your Content

One well-produced video can be repurposed into a month of content: cut into short clips, embedded in blog posts, included in email newsletters, used as video ads, and posted across multiple platforms. It's the highest-leverage content format available.

How to Start Without a Big Budget

You don't need to spend $10,000 on a brand film to get started. Here's a practical entry point for Perth businesses:

  • Start with a single testimonial video: Film a happy client on your phone (or hire a videographer for a half-day) and have it professionally edited. Budget: $500–$1,500.
  • Create a 60-second "who we are" intro: A short talking-head video introducing your team builds enormous trust on your homepage and LinkedIn.
  • Repurpose existing content: Have old photos, presentations, or screen recordings? These can be edited into engaging video content cheaply.
  • Batch your shoots: Instead of one video per month, shoot five videos in a single afternoon. The editing can be done over time, keeping your content calendar full for months.

The Cost of Waiting

Every month you don't publish video content is a month your competitors are building an advantage. Perth's business community is active and increasingly competitive — in real estate, professional services, trades, retail, and hospitality. The businesses investing in video now are capturing search traffic, social reach, and buyer trust that's very hard to take back once a competitor has established it.

The best time to start was 12 months ago. The second best time is now.
