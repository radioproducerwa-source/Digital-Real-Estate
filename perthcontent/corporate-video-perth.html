What Is Corporate Video Production?

Corporate video production covers any professionally produced video content created for a business context — internal or external. That includes brand films that tell your company story, testimonial videos that build trust with prospects, explainer videos that clarify what you do, training content that onboards staff faster, and event highlights that extend the life of your conferences and product launches.

In Perth's competitive B2B and B2C markets, video has moved from a "nice to have" to an essential communication tool. Whether you are pitching a mining services contract, attracting hospitality talent, or showcasing a new development to investors, professionally produced video communicates credibility faster than any other medium.

Perth Content connects Perth businesses with experienced editors and production coordinators who understand the local market, your industry's tone, and how to turn raw footage into content that genuinely performs.

Types of Corporate Video We Produce

Brand films are 2–5 minute flagship pieces that capture who your business is, what you stand for, and why clients should choose you. They are ideal for website homepages, trade show screens, and LinkedIn company pages. A well-crafted brand film can anchor your entire marketing strategy.

Testimonial videos place real clients on camera to describe their experience with your business. Research consistently shows that video testimonials convert at significantly higher rates than written reviews. See our dedicated testimonial video Perth service for full details.

Training and onboarding videos reduce the time and cost of bringing new staff up to speed, and can be hosted on your LMS or internal intranet. For more detail, visit our training video production Perth page. Explainer videos, conference highlights, and product demos round out the suite of corporate formats we handle every week.

The ROI of Corporate Video in Perth

Businesses that invest in professional video production routinely report measurable returns. Website pages with embedded video retain visitors longer and rank more competitively in Google's local search results. LinkedIn posts featuring native video generate, on average, five times more engagement than text-only updates. For Perth businesses targeting the resources, construction, professional services, and hospitality sectors, video is increasingly the expected standard — not a differentiator.

Even modest investments in corporate video can deliver outsized results. A single well-produced brand film, repurposed across your website, social channels, and sales presentations, can replace dozens of hours of in-person pitching. The unit cost per impression drops quickly once the asset exists.

Perth Content helps clients think about video as a strategic asset, not a one-off expense. We advise on content formats that can be repurposed across multiple channels to maximise your production budget.

What's Included in a Corporate Video Package

Our corporate video packages are scoped to your brief. A typical package includes: initial creative brief and script review, colour grading and exposure correction, sound mix and audio enhancement, lower-thirds and title cards, licensed background music, up to two rounds of revisions, and delivery in your preferred format (MP4, MOV, broadcast spec).

Larger packages may include full scriptwriting, motion graphics and animated logo idents, multi-camera editing, subtitles and closed captions for accessibility compliance, and thumbnail creation for YouTube or LinkedIn. We work with your in-house footage or can coordinate Perth-based videographers for shoots you have not yet filmed.

Every project is managed through a clear brief-to-delivery workflow with milestone sign-offs, so there are no surprises on delivery day.

Corporate Video Pricing in Perth

Corporate video editing and production in Perth typically ranges from $500 for a simple edit (colour grade, music, titles on existing footage) through to $5,000 or more for full-service brand films involving scriptwriting, motion graphics, multi-camera editing, and broadcast-quality delivery. Training video series and multi-episode content attract volume discounts.

The biggest cost drivers are footage complexity (number of cameras, event vs. controlled environment), post-production elements (animation, motion graphics, green screen compositing), revision rounds, and delivery timeline. Rush turnarounds of 24–48 hours are available for an additional fee.

We provide fixed-price quotes after reviewing your brief so you know exactly what you are spending before work begins. No hidden fees, no scope creep surprises.
