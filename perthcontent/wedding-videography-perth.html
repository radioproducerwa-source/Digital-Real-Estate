Cinematic vs. Documentary Wedding Style

Cinematic wedding films are edited like short films — with a carefully crafted narrative arc, musical scoring, colour grading that creates a consistent visual mood, and pacing that prioritises emotion over chronological completeness. A cinematic highlight film is typically 5–10 minutes long and is designed to be watched repeatedly by the couple and shared with family and friends. It does not document everything that happened; it captures how the day felt.

Documentary-style wedding video takes a more complete record of the day — ceremony from start to finish, speeches in full, key reception moments — with less editorial intervention. Documentary style is preferred by couples who want the completeness of a full record over the polished emotion of a shorter cinematic piece. Full-day documentary edits typically run 60–120 minutes and are the reference document couples return to when they want to relive the complete timeline of their day.

Many Perth couples now choose both: a 5–8 minute cinematic highlight film for sharing on social media and with family, plus a complete documentary edit for the archive. Perth Content can produce both formats from the same raw footage at a combined rate.

What's Typically Filmed at a Perth Wedding

A standard Perth wedding coverage captures: bridal preparations (getting ready footage), the ceremony (walk, vows, exchange of rings, first kiss, signing, exit), couple portraits (garden, venue grounds, golden hour), cocktail hour (guests mingling, candid moments), reception formalities (introductions, speeches, first dance, cake cutting, parent dances), and reception atmosphere (dancing, table moments, late-night energy).

Perth's wedding venues provide diverse visual environments: Swan Valley wineries with golden vineyard backdrops, Cottesloe and City Beach for ocean sunset portraits, Kings Park for iconic skyline and botanical framing, and dozens of premium function venues across the metro area including Sandalford Estate, Fraser's Restaurant, and Frasers King's Park. Our editors are experienced with the visual language and lighting conditions of Perth's most popular venues.

Drone coverage (subject to venue and airspace approval) adds spectacular aerial perspectives particularly suited to vineyard, coastal, and estate venues. Perth Content can coordinate CASA-licensed drone operators as an add-on to any wedding edit package.

Highlight Film vs. Full Day Edit

The highlight film (4–10 minutes) is the shareable, emotional centrepiece of your wedding video package. It is what couples post on Instagram, send to friends who couldn't attend, and watch on anniversaries. It is edited to music, uses the best 5–10% of all footage, and prioritises emotional resonance over chronological completeness.

The full ceremony edit captures the entire ceremony from entry to exit — uncut or with minimal trimming — in a standalone 20–45 minute video. This is invaluable for family members who couldn't attend in person and for couples who want an exact record of their vows and readings. Speech edits (trimmed and colour-graded versions of each reception speech) are often produced as a separate deliverable that guests and family particularly love to receive.

The full-day documentary edit combines all footage into a chronological 60–120 minute record. This format is more archival than cinematic and suits couples who prioritise completeness. It is rarely watched all the way through but serves as an invaluable record when specific moments are wanted years later.

Same-Day Edit and Turnaround Times

Same-day edits — a 2–3 minute highlight reel delivered at the reception, typically played in the last hour of the night — are available for Perth weddings with sufficient advance coordination. This service requires pre-approved music selections, a clear edit brief, and a photographer or second shooter delivering key footage to the editor on the day. It is a premium service that creates an unforgettable reception moment.

Standard turnaround for wedding video editing is 4–8 weeks from footage receipt, with the exact timeline depending on peak season demand and package type. Perth Content delivers milestone previews (rough cut approval before final delivery) so couples are never left waiting without visibility into the progress of their edit.

Music Licensing for Perth Wedding Videos

Music is the emotional engine of a wedding film, and music licensing for wedding videos is a legal requirement that is often misunderstood. Perth Content uses fully licensed music from reputable libraries (Musicbed, Artlist, Epidemic Sound) for all wedding highlight films, ensuring your video can be shared publicly on YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook without copyright strikes or muting. If a couple has a specific licensed song they want used (for example, their first dance song), we can advise on the licensing path required for legal use in the final film.
