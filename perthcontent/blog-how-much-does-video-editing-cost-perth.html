How Much Does Video Editing Cost in Perth in 2025?

A plain-English price guide — from $80 social media clips to $3,000 wedding films — so you can budget your next video project with confidence.

📅 1 April 2025 🏷 Pricing ⏱ 7 min read

One of the most common questions Perth businesses and individuals ask before commissioning video content is: how much is this going to cost? The honest answer is that video editing prices vary enormously depending on the type of video, its length, complexity, and turnaround time. This guide breaks it all down so you can plan your budget — and know what to expect from quotes you receive.

What Affects the Cost of Video Editing in Perth?

Before diving into specific price ranges, it helps to understand the key factors that drive editing costs:

  • Footage volume: More raw footage = more time to review, select, and assemble
  • Edit complexity: Simple cuts vs. colour grading, motion graphics, and audio mixing
  • Turnaround time: Rush jobs (24–48 hrs) typically attract a premium
  • Number of revisions: Some editors include 2 rounds; others charge per change
  • Music licensing: Royalty-free tracks vs. licensed commercial music
  • Deliverable formats: Single export vs. multiple platform-optimised versions

With those variables in mind, here are typical price ranges across the four main video types Perth Content handles.

Real Estate Video Editing Costs in Perth

Real estate video editing is one of the most in-demand services in Perth, particularly as the property market remains competitive and buyers increasingly expect video walkthroughs before inspecting in person.

  • Standard property walkthrough edit: $150 – $300
  • Walkthrough + drone footage edit: $250 – $450
  • Agent intro/outro reel: $80 – $180
  • Full listing package (walkthrough + drone + agent reel): $350 – $600
  • Luxury/prestige property (extended, colour-graded, music-licensed): $500 – $900+

Most real estate edits are delivered within 24–48 hours when footage is submitted by midday. See our real estate video editing service for full details.

Social Media Video Editing Costs in Perth

Short-form video for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts is the fastest-growing segment of video editing work in Perth. Prices reflect the shorter deliverable length but can rise quickly with added complexity (animated captions, transitions, split-screen edits).

  • Single Reel or TikTok (30–60 sec, basic edit): $80 – $150
  • Single Reel with captions + music + branded template: $120 – $220
  • YouTube Short (60 sec, full edit): $100 – $200
  • Monthly content package (8–12 videos/month): $600 – $1,400/month
  • One shoot repurposed across 3 platforms: $250 – $450

Ongoing monthly retainers offer the best value for businesses publishing video regularly. Explore our social media video service.

Corporate Video Editing Costs in Perth

Corporate video is a broad category covering everything from staff testimonials to full brand films. Complexity varies more here than in any other category, so price ranges are wider.

  • Testimonial video (2–3 min, single subject): $250 – $500
  • Explainer video (2 min, with motion graphics): $600 – $1,400
  • Training or onboarding video (5–10 min): $500 – $1,200
  • Brand promo video (60–90 sec, polished): $800 – $2,000
  • Conference or event recap (5–10 min multi-cam): $600 – $1,500

Corporate video editing typically includes 2 rounds of revisions and delivery in your required format (MP4, MOV, web-optimised). See our corporate video service.

Wedding & Event Video Editing Costs in Perth

Wedding video editing is the most personal — and often most emotionally significant — type of video project. Prices reflect the care and skill required to craft a cinematic film from a full day's footage.

  • Short highlight reel (3–5 min): $400 – $800
  • Full wedding film (20–40 min, ceremony to reception): $1,000 – $2,500
  • Same-day edit (delivered at the reception): $600 – $1,200
  • Social media highlight (60–90 sec): $200 – $400
  • Full film + highlight + social package: $1,500 – $3,500

Most Perth couples use a combination — a short social media highlight for sharing immediately and a longer film for keeping forever. See our wedding video editing service.

DIY vs. Professional Video Editing: Is It Worth Paying?

With tools like CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, and iMovie available for free, it's tempting to ask whether professional editing is worth the cost. The honest answer depends on your use case:

  • For personal use or simple clips: DIY is often fine
  • For real estate listings: Professional editing demonstrably increases enquiry rates — the ROI is clear
  • For brand-building social media: Consistent, high-quality editing builds credibility that DIY typically can't match
  • For corporate video: Poor production quality can actively damage your brand
  • For weddings: These moments can't be re-shot — professional editing is almost always worth it

How to Get Value for Money on Video Editing in Perth

A few tips to make your budget go further:

  • Brief clearly: The more detail you give your editor upfront, the fewer revision rounds you'll need
  • Shoot efficiently: Good footage = faster editing = lower cost
  • Bundle projects: Monthly content packages are significantly cheaper per video than one-off commissions
  • Plan your turnaround: Avoid rush fees by giving at least 3–5 business days where possible
  • Ask about packages: Many editors (including Perth Content's network) offer discounts for ongoing work
