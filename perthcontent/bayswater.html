Video Content Services in Bayswater

Bayswater sits approximately seven kilometres north-east of the Perth CBD, positioned between Morley and the Swan River foreshore. For years it sat quietly in the shadow of neighbouring Mount Lawley and Maylands, but the past five years have seen the suburb emerge as a genuine destination for food, coffee, and community. King William Street and the surrounding precinct have attracted independent cafes, neighbourhood restaurants, specialty retailers, and boutique service businesses that are building a local identity Bayswater residents are genuinely proud of.

Alongside this commercial awakening, Bayswater's real estate market has been among the strongest performers in the inner-northern corridor. Heritage character homes on generous blocks have attracted buyers priced out of nearby Mount Lawley and Inglewood, while a wave of infill development — townhouses, grouped dwellings, and apartment projects — has brought new residents and new investment into the suburb. This combination of organic community growth and deliberate development makes Bayswater one of the most interesting and active video content markets in Perth right now.

Perth Content works with Bayswater businesses and property professionals to produce content that captures this momentum.
