Why LinkedIn Video Outperforms Images and Text

LinkedIn's algorithm consistently privileges native video — video uploaded directly to the platform rather than linked from YouTube or Vimeo. Native LinkedIn video autoplay in the feed, receives higher organic reach, and generates significantly more comments, shares, and profile visits than equivalent text or image posts. For Perth professionals and B2B businesses, this algorithmic advantage makes video the highest-return content format on the platform.

LinkedIn's user base in Perth includes decision-makers across mining services, construction, professional services, technology, health, and education — the sectors that dominate Western Australia's economy. Reaching those decision-makers organically through well-produced video content, rather than through expensive InMail campaigns or display advertising, represents a significant competitive advantage for Perth B2B businesses willing to invest in consistent video production.

The professionals who show up consistently with quality video content on LinkedIn become the recognised thought leaders in their Perth niche. That recognition translates directly into inbound leads, speaking invitations, partnership opportunities, and hiring attraction.

LinkedIn Video Content Types for Perth Businesses

Thought leadership videos position the individual or company as an expert voice in their field. A Perth mining consultant sharing a 60-second opinion on current industry conditions, a Perth CFO explaining a financial reporting change, or a Perth HR leader discussing talent acquisition trends — these are genuinely valuable to LinkedIn's professional audience and reward consistent creators with growing reach and follower acquisition.

Behind-the-scenes and culture videos are among the most highly engaged content types on LinkedIn because they humanise businesses and show the reality of workplace life. Perth companies using behind-the-scenes video to showcase their culture see significant benefits in recruitment attraction and client relationship building.

Client testimonial videos are exceptionally powerful on LinkedIn because they are peer-to-peer. A Perth client explaining how your business solved their problem, delivered in 60–90 seconds, is far more credible than any claim you could make yourself. For a dedicated testimonial video service, see our testimonial video Perth page. Company announcement and milestone videos — new hires, project completions, award wins, office openings — engage your network and create shareable moments in your company's public narrative.

Optimal Length and Format for LinkedIn Video

LinkedIn's own data and creator community research consistently points to 30–90 seconds as the optimal length for LinkedIn video posts. Videos in this range have the highest average view completion rate, which LinkedIn rewards with broader reach distribution. Videos above 3 minutes see sharp engagement drops for most B2B content types (though long-form is appropriate for detailed tutorials or keynote recordings in certain professional contexts).

Format recommendations: landscape (16:9) for desktop-first audiences, square (1:1) for broader feed compatibility, portrait (4:5 or 9:16) for maximum screen real estate on mobile. Perth Content delivers LinkedIn video in the format that best matches your audience's typical device usage pattern and the visual content of the video itself.

Opening hooks on LinkedIn should be text-based and on-screen within the first 2 seconds, since LinkedIn videos often begin playing silently in the feed before a viewer actively chooses to watch. A strong visual hook with an on-screen text question or claim drives the click to unmute and watch.

Captions Are Non-Negotiable on LinkedIn

A significant proportion of LinkedIn video is watched without sound — either because the viewer is in a workplace, public transport, or meeting context where audio is inappropriate, or simply because they scroll without unmuting. Captions are therefore not just an accessibility feature but a fundamental performance tool: captioned LinkedIn videos consistently achieve higher completion rates and engagement than uncaptioned videos.

Perth Content includes professional closed captions on all LinkedIn video edits — not auto-generated subtitles that require review, but properly timed, accurately transcribed captions that match the speaker's pace and include appropriate punctuation and formatting. We also offer open-caption (burned-in text) versions for platforms where closed caption display is unreliable.

Personal Brand vs. Company Page: Perth LinkedIn Strategy

Individual LinkedIn profiles consistently outperform company pages on almost every organic reach metric. LinkedIn's algorithm favours person-to-person content over brand broadcasting. This means that for most Perth B2B businesses, the highest-return LinkedIn video strategy is to produce content for the personal profiles of senior team members — the CEO, the founder, the practice leads — rather than for the company page alone.

Company page video still serves an important function as a repository of brand content and a destination for external audiences doing due diligence. But the growth engine is personal profiles, and Perth Content works with individual Perth professionals to develop a consistent personal brand video strategy that builds authority and generates enquiry.
