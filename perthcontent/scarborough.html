Video Content Services in Scarborough

Scarborough Beach is one of Perth's defining landmarks — a kilometre of white sand backed by a revitalised foreshore precinct that draws hundreds of thousands of visitors each year. Since the completion of the Scarborough Beach Redevelopment in 2018, the suburb has transformed into a genuine destination for dining, nightlife, fitness, and coastal lifestyle, drawing a young, active, and digitally-savvy demographic who document and share their experiences constantly.

For businesses operating in this environment, video content isn't optional — it's the language of the market. Perth Content works with Scarborough's restaurants, bars, surf schools, fitness studios, accommodation providers, tourism operators, and lifestyle brands to produce content that performs in feeds saturated with beach aesthetics. Our editors know how to capture the golden-hour glow on the water, the energy of a packed rooftop bar, and the joy of a surf lesson — and translate that into content people actually watch to the end.

Beyond the foreshore, Scarborough's residential streets are home to a growing community of young professionals and families who increasingly expect their local businesses to show up professionally online. We help cafes, health and wellness studios, and service businesses across the broader Scarborough area build social audiences through consistent, high-quality short-form video.

Our process is simple: brief us on your goals, supply your footage or coordinate with a videographer we trust, and receive a polished final edit within 48–72 hours. We work around your schedule and deliver content in every format you need — from full-resolution versions for paid ads through to vertical cuts for Stories and Reels.

Properties here sell on lifestyle appeal as much as square meterage — and video is the perfect medium to convey that.

Perth Content produces listing videos for Scarborough properties that lead with the lifestyle: the ocean views, the walk to the beach, the proximity to the foreshore dining precinct.
